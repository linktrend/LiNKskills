# Advanced planning guidance

## Horizon discipline

Use one horizon per artifact. A rolling four-week review is not a monthly plan;
an annual target is not a five-year forecast. For three-year and five-year
inputs, prefer directional labels unless supplied evidence includes a defined
measurement method, period, unit, and confidence.

## KPI comparison

Keep target, forecast, and actual as separate values. A variance is comparable
only when the values use the same unit and period and point to supplied
evidence. If one value is absent, mark `NOT_COMPARABLE` and explain the gap.
Do not convert currencies, infer denominators, smooth missing observations, or
round away material uncertainty.

## Delivery signals

`late`, `blocked`, `obsolete`, and `on_track` are supplied signals, not model
judgments. Preserve the signal reference and note. Do not turn a signal into a
task, escalation, schedule, status mutation, or completion claim.

## Reprioritization

Require a proposed status, rationale, affected objective references, and an
evidence reference. A proposed reprioritization is ready for owner review only;
it cannot change a Program, project, milestone, objective, KPI, or Task.
