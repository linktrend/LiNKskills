# Old patterns to reject

- Hardcoded personal schedules, thresholds, charger names, locations, or reminders.
- Treating a missing selfie or silent user as completion or consent.
- Updating a confirmed image value in place without correction history.
- Mixing charge and discharge rates or ignoring saturation near the upper target.
- Sending reminders, writing a private ledger, reading a device, or cancelling maintenance.
- Putting real private values, image bytes, identifiers, or health conclusions in fixtures.
- Diagnosing, changing treatment, or copying consumer bindings into a reusable release.
