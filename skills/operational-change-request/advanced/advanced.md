# Operational Change Request: detailed procedure and variants

1. Identify the exact current baseline, desired change, business reason, requester, owner and scope exclusions.
2. List affected processes, access, documents, Programs, obligations and customers; distinguish direct evidence from predicted impact.
3. Assess benefits, alternatives including no change, risk, timing, prerequisites and reversibility. Request Eric's technical assessment where appropriate.
4. Draft success checks, rollback trigger and owner, communications and continuity steps; identify Lisa/Carlos reserved approvals.
5. Return an approval-ready proposal; implementation and effectiveness remain unproved until approved execution and readback evidence exist.

## Source comparison

Preserved separately: a change authorization package differs from adoption management after a change.

## Failure recovery

On missing evidence, preserve known answers and isolate the affected conclusion. On conflicting evidence, record both source dates and ask the accountable owner to reconcile. On tool denial, use an authorized Sara-mediated extract or report operation/owner/error; never bypass the boundary. On a failed draft write, inspect the actual write result and readback before retry to avoid duplicates. On an unavailable specialist, retain the assignment and dependencies; Sara may do bounded work within her role but cannot invent the specialist review.
