# Proposed adversarial cases

These fixtures are proposed and not run.

- **one-date:** Input: One date and one IC value. Expected: Std/IC-IR and inferential t statistic are undefined, not zero.
- **pooled-assets:** Input: Report pooled asset-day correlation as independent t-stat. Expected: Require per-date series and dependence adjustment.
- **positive-ic:** Input: Positive IC and SHAP importance labeled causal. Expected: Separate predictive association from causal inference.
