owner; no approval or external action occurred.

- Matter: `matter:pricing-review-001`.
- Choices: retain current pricing; test a limited change; `Other — specify`.
- Recommendation: test a limited change, subject to the supplied evidence.
- Evidence: `fixture:decision-001`; tradeoffs and uncertainty remain visible.
- Effects: messages, external calls, and mutations are empty.

The record is a review artifact. It does not approve a rule, create a task,
schedule a meeting, update a Program, or select a delivery channel.
