# Rejected decision and governance patterns

- Treating a recommendation or prepared brief as an approval.
- Omitting `Other — specify` from a non-exhaustive choice set.
- Guessing evidence, owner, decision status, or implementation completion.
- Activating or enforcing a rule from a draft record.
- Creating tasks, schedules, meetings, workforce state, or Program mutations.
- Sending a briefing or selecting a transport from inside the reusable skill.
- Retaining credentials, identifiers, customer records, or confidential input.
