# Vendor Performance Review: detailed procedure and variants

1. Confirm existing-vendor scope, as-of date, reviewed period, owner, contract end/renewal terms, spend and business dependency. Initial vendor selection and contract drafting use their distinct skills.
2. Define transparent dimensions and owner-approved weights for reliability, support, security, commercial flexibility and strategic fit. Score actual uptime, incidents, P90 support, current evidence and alternatives; missing inputs remain unscored, not zero. Upstream industry weights and KEEP/REPLACE thresholds are examples, not adopted company policy.
3. Compare actual SLA metrics with signed targets and prior periods, respecting direction and compatible windows. Determine credit eligibility from contractual breaches, claim conditions, deadline and cap; the upstream two-breach or 0.5-point heuristic does not establish legal entitlement.
4. Classify data sensitivity, financial exposure, operational dependency and applicable regulatory exposure separately. Verify certifications rather than trust questionnaire claims, assess break-glass/exit feasibility and flag unsupported regulatory assumptions.
5. Combine the scorecard, SLA trend and risk findings into bounded keep/review/replace proposals with reason, confidence, review owner, mitigations and renewal/exit dependencies. Claim submissions, notices, offboarding, access changes and purchases require their actual authority; this skill produces drafts, not execution permission.

## Failure recovery

On missing evidence, preserve known answers and isolate the affected conclusion. On conflicting evidence, record both source dates and ask the accountable owner to reconcile. On tool denial, use an authorized Sara-mediated extract or report operation/owner/error; never bypass the boundary. On a failed draft write, inspect the actual write result and readback before retry to avoid duplicates. On an unavailable specialist, retain the assignment and dependencies; Sara may do bounded work within her role but cannot invent the specialist review.
