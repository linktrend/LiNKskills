# Task-specific method cards

Use only the branch matching the requested deliverable. Preserve the full source procedures under `upstream/`; this concise card index is not a substitute for the substantive Phase 2 method.

- **Branch 1:** Define one HR process, owner, start/end event, population and intended service outcome; do not pull in unrelated HR programs.
- **Branch 2:** Map actors, inputs, decisions, systems, handoffs, waiting time and outputs from supplied records or interviews. Mark undocumented steps as reported, not verified.
- **Branch 3:** Define throughput, cycle time, first-pass yield, rework and cost/capacity only when source data supports each. Calculate baseline with denominator, period, sample size and exclusions visible.
- **Branch 4:** Compare baseline with the owner-supplied goal and constraints. If no target exists, show the baseline and plausible options without declaring a performance gap.
- **Branch 5:** Use 5 Whys or a fishbone only to organize evidence-backed hypotheses; distinguish correlation and anecdote from established cause.
- **Branch 6:** Model capacity as required volume × observed time per unit versus available staffed time × supplied productivity factor; expose utilization and coverage assumptions.
- **Branch 7:** Offer a small reversible pilot with success measures, guardrails, owner, duration and rollback conditions. Do not execute the pilot, change HRIS, or notify staff.
- **Branch 8:** Deliver a process map, metric table, options, risks, evidence gaps and decision owner. Route policy/legal questions to current jurisdiction-specific counsel.
