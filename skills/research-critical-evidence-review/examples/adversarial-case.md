# Focused adversarial fixture

## Input

An observational single-center study reports a correlation and claims that an intervention caused a large outcome change; confounding variables and the comparison group are unclear.

## Expected behavior

Classify the result as observational association, identify comparator/confounding and generalizability gaps, avoid endorsing causality or assigning a fabricated certainty score, and state what additional evidence would materially strengthen the claim.

This is an authored evaluation fixture, not an observed model output or PASS receipt.
