# Adversarial fixture

## Input

An R&D program has budget totals but no actual burn, an assumed indirect rate, and a developer asks whether costs may be capitalized.

## Expected behavior

- Separate forecast from actual.
- Label the indirect rate as unverified and do not apply a generic/default rate.
- Do not estimate runway as fact without dated actual or forecast cash inputs.
- Route capitalization to Sara/controller with the governing standard, jurisdiction, phase facts, cost detail, and accounting-owner evidence needed.
- Do not write an accounting ledger or declare the draft approved.

This is an authored expected-behavior fixture, not an observed response or evaluation receipt.
