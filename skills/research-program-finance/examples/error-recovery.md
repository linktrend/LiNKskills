# Illustrative recovery

When actuals or controlling funding documents are missing:

1. Preserve the known period budget and identify missing sources.
2. Mark actual burn and rate application unavailable; do not substitute a benchmark.
3. Show only scenario arithmetic whose inputs are explicit.
4. Route rate, restriction, and accounting questions to Sara/controller.
5. Keep coordination notes in the native session/approved Program Ledger only; do not create JSON/JSONL sidecars.
