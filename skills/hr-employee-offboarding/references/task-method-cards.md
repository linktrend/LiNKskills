# Task-specific method cards

Use only the branch matching the requested deliverable. Preserve the full source procedures under `upstream/`; this concise card index is not a substitute for the substantive Phase 2 method.

- **Branch 1:** Confirm the requester is authorized to prepare this plan and source the effective date/status from approved records. If status is disputed or termination is contemplated, route to employee relations/counsel; do not decide.
- **Branch 2:** Separate employee-facing conversation guidance, internal task coordination, knowledge transfer, equipment and access owner tasks, payroll/benefits checklist, and records retention. Note each owner and due date from supplied policy.
- **Branch 3:** Create a role knowledge-transfer inventory: recurring duties, current work, key process references, decisions due, dependencies, and receiving owner. Avoid copying unnecessary confidential or personal content.
- **Branch 4:** Prepare property/access removal requests as tasks for authorized owners. Never change access, contact providers, cancel accounts, send notices or make payroll changes.
- **Branch 5:** If an exit interview is requested, state who will see responses, voluntary nature if established by policy, limits to confidentiality and retention source; ask neutral questions without promising secrecy.
- **Branch 6:** Flag final pay, benefits continuation, leave, notice, immigration, noncompete and record-retention questions for jurisdiction-specific review using current primary authorities. Do not state deadlines or legal entitlements from memory.
- **Branch 7:** Report completed checklist sections, owner status, open items and a continuity risk list. Separate voluntary feedback from formal investigation and route allegations to ER.
