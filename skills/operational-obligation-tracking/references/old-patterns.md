# Known-bad patterns

- Department packaging that loses distinct tasks.
- Source instructions treated as company authority, approval or current legal rules.
- Invented baselines, zeros, deadlines, metrics, permissions or tool names.
- Blanket approval for harmless evidence gathering and drafting.
- Claiming behavioral qualification from a format check or entrypoint read.
- Private raw evidence in shared telemetry or runtime JSONL sidecars.
- Ticket closure presented as integration, adoption or effectiveness proof.
