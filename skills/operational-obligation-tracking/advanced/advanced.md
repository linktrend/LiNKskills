# Operational Obligation Tracking: detailed procedure and variants

1. Identify each obligation's actual source, edition/effective date, jurisdiction or contract scope and required company facts.
2. Separate mandatory requirements, contractual terms, approved policy, voluntary standard and proposed good practice; a generic template is not authority.
3. Record applicability as established, conditional, conflicting or unknown, with explicit missing facts and responsible reviewer.
4. For applicable obligations record action, accountable owner, due-date basis, evidence location, dependencies, recurrence and escalation trigger; never invent statutory dates.
5. Compare due work with actual completion evidence and produce overdue/unknown items. Sending filings or declaring compliance is outside this tracking task.

## Failure recovery

On missing evidence, preserve known answers and isolate the affected conclusion. On conflicting evidence, record both source dates and ask the accountable owner to reconcile. On tool denial, use an authorized Sara-mediated extract or report operation/owner/error; never bypass the boundary. On a failed draft write, inspect the actual write result and readback before retry to avoid duplicates. On an unavailable specialist, retain the assignment and dependencies; Sara may do bounded work within her role but cannot invent the specialist review.
