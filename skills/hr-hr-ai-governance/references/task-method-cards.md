# Task-specific method cards

Use only the branch matching the requested deliverable. Preserve the full source procedures under `upstream/`; this concise card index is not a substitute for the substantive Phase 2 method.

- **Branch 1:** Inventory each AI use case separately: purpose, users, affected people, system/vendor, data, output, decision dependency and accountable owner. Include pilots and shadow uses if records show them.
- **Branch 2:** Classify impact using only supplied approved policy. If none exists, propose a review tier as a draft and label it; do not declare legal risk categories or compliance status.
- **Branch 3:** Map controls by lifecycle: intake and approval, procurement, data minimization/quality, testing, human review/appeal, deployment monitoring, incident response, model/vendor changes, retention and retirement.
- **Branch 4:** For consequential employment use, require documented human accountability, job-related basis, meaningful review ability and a non-AI fallback in the proposal. Do not infer law or claim human review is legally sufficient.
- **Branch 5:** Define evidence owner and record for each control: version, evaluation, subgroup outcomes, overrides, complaints, incidents, access and review cadence. Set no numeric threshold without approved policy or current authority.
- **Branch 6:** Identify changes that require reassessment (model/version, purpose, data, population, vendor terms, decision role) and route privacy/security/legal questions to named owners.
- **Branch 7:** Prepare policy/process draft with open decisions, responsibilities and escalation contacts. Do not configure systems, authorize deployment, or write official policy.
