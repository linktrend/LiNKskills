# Task-specific method cards

Use only the branch matching the requested deliverable. Preserve the full source procedures under `upstream/`; this concise card index is not a substitute for the substantive Phase 2 method.

- **Branch 1:** Define the program question, population, locations, current design and decision owner. Separate compensation-program architecture from individual pay benchmarking, offer drafting and benefits enrollment.
- **Branch 2:** Inventory existing components: base pay, variable pay, equity, health/wellbeing, retirement, leave, recognition and mobility only where evidence is supplied. Distinguish policy text, actual practice and proposed design.
- **Branch 3:** Identify design objectives (attraction, retention, fairness, affordability, simplicity) and translate to owner-approved criteria. Do not use source generic benchmarks or assume a legal minimum.
- **Branch 4:** Compare options by eligibility, employee experience, estimated cost, administration, equity effects and dependencies. State formulas, population assumptions and missing data; model scenarios only with supplied rates/counts.
- **Branch 5:** For benefits/tax/leave rules, identify location and plan year and verify current official authority or request broker/counsel review. Keep legal/tax conclusions separate from business choices.
- **Branch 6:** Check interactions across components (base/bonus/equity, employer cost, leave and retirement) and identify unintended incentives or unequal access using authorized aggregate data.
- **Branch 7:** Draft program terms or decision brief with unresolved policy choices, review owners and communication outline. Do not set pay, enroll employees, change plans, or send announcements.
