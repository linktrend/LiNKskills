# Task-specific method cards

Use only the branch matching the requested deliverable. Preserve the full source procedures under `upstream/`; this concise card index is not a substitute for the substantive Phase 2 method.

- **Branch 1:** Check whether there is an immediate safety threat, retaliation risk, harassment allegation, legal hold or deadline known from authorized records. If yes, route promptly to the named ER/counsel/safety owner; do not investigate beyond safe preservation.
- **Branch 2:** Limit scope and access to authorized need-to-know roles. Pseudonymize notes in reusable artifacts and keep sensitive evidence in approved restricted records.
- **Branch 3:** Separate allegation, first-hand observation, document, inference, response and unknown. Build a dated timeline and list contradictions without resolving them prematurely.
- **Branch 4:** Prepare a neutral evidence plan: relevant policy, records, witnesses, questions and sequencing. Avoid leading questions; offer each relevant person a fair chance to respond through the authorized process.
- **Branch 5:** Keep contemporaneous notes with source/date, speaker attribution and exact material language only when needed. Do not infer credibility from demeanor, protected traits or inconsistent memory alone.
- **Branch 6:** Compare facts to supplied policy wording; if legal analysis is needed, obtain current jurisdiction-specific primary sources and counsel review. Do not decide misconduct, credibility, discipline or employment outcome.
- **Branch 7:** Return current facts, open questions, risk/urgency flags, requested owner decision and a respectful follow-up plan. No employee contact, findings letter or case-system changes.
