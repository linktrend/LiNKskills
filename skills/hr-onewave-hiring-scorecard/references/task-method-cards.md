# Task-specific method cards

Use only the branch matching the requested deliverable. Preserve the full source procedures under `upstream/`; this concise card index is not a substitute for the substantive Phase 2 method.

- **Branch 1:** Confirm role, level, job outcomes, interview stages and owner. If the requisition or criteria are not approved, label the scorecard a draft proposal.
- **Branch 2:** Translate each must-have outcome into specific observable evidence and a verification method. Separate essential criteria from preferences and remove vague “culture fit” or personality proxies.
- **Branch 3:** Select a role-appropriate set of competencies; use only those relevant to job outcomes. Define each competency, evidence examples and what a lower/higher anchored rating means. If the source 1–5 scale is used, label it proposed until owner adoption.
- **Branch 4:** Write consistent behavioral/situational questions per competency with follow-up probes and evidence indicators. Avoid questions about protected/personal traits and unsupported assumptions about candidate background.
- **Branch 5:** Create an independent interviewer scoresheet with evidence notes, rating, confidence/coverage and missing evidence. Do not aggregate into a hire/reject score without approved weighting and decision rule.
- **Branch 6:** Add debrief instructions that gather evidence before opinions, compare candidates against same role criteria, record dissent, and separate concerns requiring verification. Include bias checks as prompts, not proof.
- **Branch 7:** Provide reference-check questions only where authorized and lawful. Prepare one panel-ready draft with source/criteria and owner approval questions; do not contact candidates or decide selection.
