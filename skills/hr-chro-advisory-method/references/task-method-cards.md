# Task-specific method cards

Use only the branch matching the requested deliverable. Preserve the full source procedures under `upstream/`; this concise card index is not a substitute for the substantive Phase 2 method.

- **Branch 1:** Define the strategic question, accountable executive, horizon, affected organization and decision deadline. Distinguish a strategic option paper from a request to run an HR program.
- **Branch 2:** Read only approved strategy, workforce, organizational, finance and talent evidence needed for the decision. Record dates, definitions and data gaps.
- **Branch 3:** Map objective-to-capability implications (roles, critical skills, organization design, leadership capacity, talent supply) with evidence supporting each link; label hypotheses and avoid asserting causation.
- **Branch 4:** Develop at least two viable options, including a credible “hold/current approach” comparator. Compare capability impact, cost/headcount implications, implementation dependencies, risk and reversibility using supplied data.
- **Branch 5:** Stress-test the option against different business conditions and identify leading indicators that would change the recommendation. Treat external benchmarks as evidence requiring date/source/applicability, never as default targets.
- **Branch 6:** Provide a conditional recommendation and executive decision criteria; route organization redesign, workforce forecasting, compensation, employee relations, performance systems and policy drafts to the task-specific skills.
- **Branch 7:** Prepare an executive brief with decision required, evidence, options, tradeoffs, open questions, owners and proposed review points. No organization changes, employee decisions, policy adoption or external communication.
