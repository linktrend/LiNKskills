# vendor-lifecycle-review — method selection and source boundaries

Same supplier selection/renewal/exit decision task: Borghei supplies predetermined criteria, actual notice windows and lifecycle options; Anthropic adds comparable TCO and negotiation/comparison framing. Ongoing periodic scorecard and SLA-claim analysis exclusively route to vendor-performance-review.

## Integrated task controls

1. Select the requested lifecycle review scope and explicit as-of date. Collect contract owner, renewal date, notice period, auto-renew clause, actual spend, data access, business dependency and alternatives; missing terms remain unknown.
2. For a selection decision, pre-register must-have gates and weights before candidate scoring, define score anchors, cite evidence per score and calibrate scorers. Assess ranking sensitivity to reasonable weight changes; report fragile rankings or ties to the decision owner instead of adjusting weights to endorse a preferred vendor.
3. Calculate comparable total cost including license, implementation, migration, training, support and exit. Distinguish one-time/recurring horizons and unknown costs; do not substitute headline price for total cost.
4. For portfolio/renewal review, derive the actual notice deadline from contractual terms and time zone, identify locked auto-renewals and unowned contracts, assign proposed responsible owners and calendar follow-ups. Evaluate dependency and category concentration, verified security evidence and break-glass/export feasibility. Upstream percentages and schedules are examples, not company policy.
5. Use an existing independently prepared vendor-performance-review as evidence for the decision. If absent or stale, request that separate task and mark its unresolved findings. Do not rescore ongoing performance, recompute SLA credit eligibility or create a second periodic scorecard here.
6. Compare continue, renegotiate, replace or exit using verified obligations, independently reported performance/risk, alternatives, switching costs and decision deadlines. Keep notice drafts, claims, revocation and offboarding execution outside this decision task.
7. Return the evidence-backed selection/renewal/exit recommendation, sensitivity and decision options, questions and exact owner approvals. Do not send notices, change access, purchase or submit claims.

Complete source copies remain evidence; do not execute unreviewed source scripts.
