 | VENDOR ONBOARDING PROCESS
 | POLICY & PREQUALIFICATION
 |  | SET POLICY  | Work with a team to create policy based on internal and external compliance regulations and laws.
 |  | PREQUALIFICATION 
AND RISK ASSESSMENT  | Review vendors based on company needs and assess risk based on their previous compliance history and what processes they have in place.
 |  | PROCUREMENT DETAILS | Review vendor needs and structure potential contracts with those in mind.
 | SELECTION & ONBOARDING
 |  | SUPPLIER REQUIREMENTS | Further refine what company needs are for specific products or services and develop a checklist and communication protocols.
 |  | FINALIZE WRITTEN CONTRACT  | Create documents and put them through legal review; after vendor selection, review them with the vendor.
 |  | ACCOUNT SET UP | Enter the new vendor into your software systems after contracts are signed; give the vendor access to  the self-service portal and provide training.
 | PERFORMANCE AUDITING & RELATIONSHIP DEVELOPMENT
 |  | SPEND DATA 
AND ANALYTICS | Once the relationship has begun, track vendor performance.
 |  | ENSURE COMPLIANCE | Periodically check performance against any company or local, national, or international regulations and address any variance. 
 |  | TRAINING AND SUCCESS SUPPORTS | Continue training as needed and after a review of performance, 
work with the vendor to provide any further information or other supports.
 | ASSESSMENT & CONTRACT CONCLUSION
 |  | REVIEW PERFORMANCE | Collect ongoing performance data.
 |  | FINAL ASSESSMENT 
AND DATA REVIEW | Rate the vendor based on company metrics.
 |  | SHARE INFORMATION WITH VENDOR | Meet in person or virtually to communicate performance for the length of the contract.
 |  | CONCLUDE CONTRACT - RENEW OR REVISE | Close off the current contract, and decide if it should be renewed or revised before renewal.
 |  | IMPROVE PROCESSES | If discussions with the vendor, or the internal review, reveal points where there could be upgrades, meet with the internal work group and update processes.
