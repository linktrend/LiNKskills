# Operating Status Report: detailed procedure and variants

1. Read approved targets and the latest accountable-owner records; preserve each Program's authoritative Ledger ownership.
2. For each outcome record actual evidence date, current status, owner and change since the prior report. A narrative completion claim is not accepted delivery.
3. Identify stale or conflicting records, cross-domain dependencies, critical blockers, action owners and the decision actually required.
4. Summarize measured results, outlook and capacity/financial implications with units and uncertainties. Do not fill missing metrics with zero.
5. Return a concise review with action/decision/exception registers and next check. Sara supplies Lisa's executive briefing inputs; do not create a competing CEO report schedule.

## Failure recovery

On missing evidence, preserve known answers and isolate the affected conclusion. On conflicting evidence, record both source dates and ask the accountable owner to reconcile. On tool denial, use an authorized Sara-mediated extract or report operation/owner/error; never bypass the boundary. On a failed draft write, inspect the actual write result and readback before retry to avoid duplicates. On an unavailable specialist, retain the assignment and dependencies; Sara may do bounded work within her role but cannot invent the specialist review.
