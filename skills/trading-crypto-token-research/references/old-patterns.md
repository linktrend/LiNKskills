# Excluded source patterns

- Generic token thresholds presented as calibrated price-impact or valuation rules.
- FDV/protocol gross activity described as token-holder earnings without evidenced rights.
- Partial holder lists described as complete population, or addresses treated as beneficial owners.
- Vendor risk scores or funding clusters treated as proof of insider intent.
- Program IDs, fees, reserves, or layouts reused without exact current chain version and authoritative fixture.
- Scripts or paid providers assumed available.
