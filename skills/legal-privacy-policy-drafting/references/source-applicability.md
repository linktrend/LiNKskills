# Source applicability and merge

This draft merges two sources for the same output: a jurisdiction-aware privacy policy. The strongest base is Stephane Boghossian: jurisdiction-first intake, current-source/citation firewall, practice-to-clause reconciliation, visible gaps and QA. Malik Taiar contributes the structured controller/contact, product, data-category, purpose/basis, processor/transfer, cookie, retention and template-selection checklist.

The exact entrypoint hashes, complete original support trees and notices are preserved in `references/upstream/SOURCE-MANIFEST.json`. The sources contain legal assertions and reference materials last verified in 2026-06 or earlier; they are leads only, not current legal authority. Current primary sources must be consulted at execution. No licensing conclusion is made by this pack; exact declarations and notices are preserved for the owner.

Not in scope: drafting a privacy notice as a distinct artifact, employee/HR notice, DPA contract negotiation, DSAR response, or deciding company policy/basis/retention.
