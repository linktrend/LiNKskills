# Legal Meeting Briefing Cards

## Meeting types
- **Contract/deal review:** contract version, counterparty, term sheet, open redlines, playbook positions, deal constraint and desired negotiation outcome.
- **Board or committee:** evidence-backed decision request, current status, material risks, proposed minutes/resolution text, unresolved legal or governance requirements.
- **Compliance review:** identified obligation, source, owner, due date, evidence, gaps and remediation options.
- **Vendor/customer:** contract and performance facts, unresolved items, available alternatives and commitments that require approval.
- **Regulator/counsel:** factual timeline, supplied correspondence, questions and owner; preserve exact source context; do not characterize privilege or legal posture without authorized direction.
- **Litigation/dispute:** only when expressly assigned; organize supplied matter facts and counsel questions. Do not invent strategy or access unrelated matter content.

## Action capture
For each action, preserve the meeting decision or source note, task verb, accountable owner, due date and dependency. Mark ambiguous commitment as `proposed_action`, not decided. Distinguish `decision`, `action`, `question`, `risk` and `parking_lot`. Do not write to a project tracker without an approved action and explicit authorization.
