Use only the branch relevant to the user’s question. These methods are not execution permission and do not set policy thresholds.

## Task-specific analysis

Freeze trade identity, original contemporaneous plan, inclusion universe and maturity horizon. Reconcile partial legs/costs before net returns. Preserve raw MAE/MFE and report them with explicit direction and path convention. For R, use immutable initial side-aware risk: long risk per unit `entry-original stop`; short risk `original stop-entry`; multiply by original units/multiplier as appropriate. Later trailed stops do not redefine original R. Missing/nonpositive original risk means R is undefined.

Show mature/pending counts by horizon, benchmark and costs. Failed/rejected candidates reveal selection bias. Separate process adherence from outcome. Do not infer behavior or causality from P&L, timing or language.

## Evidence, uncertainty, and recommendations

Report primary result, strongest credible alternative explanation, and what evidence would reverse the conclusion. If inputs are incomplete, return a partial finding with exact missing fields.
