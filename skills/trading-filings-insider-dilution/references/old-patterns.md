# Failure patterns: Trading Filings, Insider and Dilution Research

- Generic thresholds presented as validated truth.
- Missing, stale, or period-mismatched evidence silently treated as zero/current.
- Results without timestamps, units, denominators, entity population, or source lineage.
- Observational association presented as causal effect.
- Proposed recommendations represented as approved actions.
- Source scripts or stale provider claims treated as proof.
