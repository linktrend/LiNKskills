# Personal Tax Strategy Scenario Analysis: task method and checks

## Trigger and boundary

Prepare a comparative worksheet for an individual's explicitly requested tax-planning scenario. This is a task draft only and is unbound pending role/owner confirmation. It does not provide tax advice, decide an election, or authorize an action.

## Required evidence

- Individual taxpayer scope, relevant jurisdictions and tax year/date.
- Goal and alternatives to compare.
- Relevant authorized account/transaction facts with source identifiers and dates.
- Current primary authority applicable to the facts and period, including effective date.
- Named external CPA/tax-counsel reviewer.

## Procedure

1. Clarify the comparison dimension (tax impact, cash/liquidity timing, or both) and requested options. Do not imply one option is universally preferable.
2. For each option, record source facts, verified authority, eligibility inputs and missing conditions. If taxpayer classification, filing/status fact, account type, holding period, basis or other determining fact is absent, keep the effect uncalculated.
3. Calculate only from the supplied/verified rule and data. Show formula, intermediate values, dates and sensitivity; separate modeled tax effects from cashflow or non-tax benefits. Preserve units and avoid false precision.
4. Identify one-time vs recurring timing only when a current source establishes it. Do not reuse the upstream source's example categories as a claim that a tax option is currently available to this person.
5. Present a neutral option table with `source fact`, `current rule and authority`, `calculation`, `assumptions`, `sensitivity`, `unknowns`, and `CPA question`. State `not calculable` when authority or facts are missing.
6. Do not call a planning option a recommendation or savings opportunity unless the actual reviewer supplies and approves that conclusion. Do not implement any choice.

## Acceptance checks

- Individual and jurisdiction/tax year scope are explicit or the affected result is withheld.
- Any stated legal/tax rule cites current primary authority and effective date.
- Numeric results reproduce from visible sourced inputs and rules; unverified elements are not filled with guessed rates, limits or eligibility.
- Tax consequence is separate from liquidity/timing scenario; no election/trade/contribution/donation/filing/payment is made.
