# Personal tax scenario method cards

These task cards adapt the pinned OneWave `tax-strategy-optimizer` source topics. They identify questions to research, not tax rules or advice. Exact original source and copyright notice are preserved under `references/upstream/`.

## Card A — Pre-tax vs Roth contribution scenario

Collect account type, contribution year, eligibility/status facts, planned contribution, current and future tax assumptions only when supplied, and relevant official authority effective for that year. Compare immediate tax treatment and future distribution treatment only after confirming the applicable rules and qualifying facts. If future rates are unknown, show neutral sensitivity inputs rather than claiming one contribution type wins. Do not initiate or recommend a contribution.

## Card B — Charitable giving scenario

Clarify the gift type, recipient, timing, asset/cost basis and taxpayer's applicable tax situation. Verify recipient/asset eligibility, substantiation and valuation rules using current primary authority. Keep charitable purpose/value separate from any tax effect. Without authority and facts, present a checklist and CPA questions only; do not suggest a gift amount or claim a deduction.

## Card C — Capital-gains timing scenario

Capture asset/account, acquisition and potential disposal dates, proceeds, documented basis, transaction costs and relevant loss/other facts. Verify current jurisdiction-specific recognition, rates, holding-period and loss-offset rules from official primary sources. Model alternative dates only when the legal treatment is sourced. Do not recommend or place a trade, sale or transfer.

## Card D — Deduction comparison

For each expense/claim, collect source evidence, taxpayer and purpose facts, tax period, amount, substantiation, and official authority for eligibility, limits and timing. Distinguish book expense, cash spend and potentially deductible amount. Do not treat a source's category list as an eligible deduction list; unknown eligibility remains an open tax-professional question.

## Common output

| Option | Source facts and dates | Primary authority/effective date | Formula/result | Assumptions/sensitivity | Missing facts | CPA question |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

When the authority or a material eligibility fact is absent, set `Formula/result` to `not calculable` and fill the missing-fact/reviewer columns. No generic tax-saving estimate is produced.
