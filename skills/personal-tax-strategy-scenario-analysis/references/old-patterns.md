# Unsafe or unsupported patterns

- Treating the upstream persona “expert tax strategist” as qualification or permission.
- Reusing generic/current-looking tax advice without jurisdiction, tax year, taxpayer facts and a current primary authority.
- Assuming Roth eligibility, deduction, charitable status, basis, holding period, rates, limits, future marginal tax rate or expected savings.
- Calling a sensitivity a recommendation, or initiating an election, trade, contribution, donation, filing or payment.
- Copying raw personal tax records into reusable skill or telemetry artifacts.
