# Synthetic example: personal tax strategy scenario intake

**Request:** A fictional individual asks whether a USD 6,000 pre-tax or Roth contribution is preferable. They have not supplied jurisdiction, tax year, taxpayer/filing status, account eligibility facts, current contribution room, current primary authority, or future tax assumptions.

**Prepared output:** List known fact `requested contribution amount: USD 6,000`; mark tax effect `not calculable`. Ask for the missing facts and obtain official current authorities effective for the year before comparison. Show a neutral blank option table with immediate-tax, future-distribution, liquidity and eligibility columns; do not fill any benefit, rate or eligibility. Route conclusions to the individual's CPA/tax counsel.

This example uses no real person and asserts no tax law.
