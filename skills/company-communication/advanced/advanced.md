# Communication quality heuristics

## Mobile and audience checks

Put the decision or status in the first two lines. Prefer one idea per bullet,
short labels, and no more than the detail needed for the audience's next step.
Technical readers may receive a compact evidence appendix; the Principal should
not receive an unexplained context dump.

## Honest status language

Use `verified`, `reported`, `proposed`, `blocked`, or `unknown` precisely. A
draft, prepared intent, test result, or recommendation is not a sent message,
approval, merge, deployment, or production result. Name the owner and the
missing evidence when a status cannot be verified.

## Choice construction

Give the smallest complete set of meaningful choices, identify tradeoffs, and
include `Other — specify` when the operator may have an option outside the
listed set. Never disguise a required human decision as a default.
