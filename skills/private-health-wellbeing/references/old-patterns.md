# Rejected health patterns

- Guessing a missing value instead of preserving `not_reported`.
- Asking a question whose stable field is already known.
- Combining energy, mood, stress, capacity, scale, waist, or bowel fields.
- Treating a nutrition or protein estimate as a target or clinical conclusion.
- Presenting an exercise proposal as a treatment or spot-reduction promise.
- Changing a dose, diagnosing, triaging, or generating emergency-support stock
  wording.
- Treating a photo classification as certain without material uncertainty and
  an append-only correction record.
- Creating a calendar reminder, exporting detailed health data, or retaining
  real personal records in a fixture, release, telemetry event, or log.
