# Known task-specific failure modes

- Treating upstream role prompts as authority or callable tools.
- Treating an unverified source, benchmark, sample threshold or jurisdictional rule as policy.
- Merging distinct HR deliverables merely because they share a broad domain.
- Concluding causation, legal compliance, individual performance or employment outcome from incomplete evidence.
- Exposing names or small-group personal data in reusable artifacts.
- Performing HRIS, ATS, payroll, policy, access or external communication actions.
