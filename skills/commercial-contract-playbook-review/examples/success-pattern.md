# Worked synthetic success example — commercial-contract-playbook-review

**Task:** playbook-or-contract-golden from this skill’s `references/eval-suite.json`.

**Case facts**
Synthetic: Complete SaaS agreement, customer side, supplied playbook requires 12-month cap with only confidentiality/IP carveouts. Clause caps ordinary breach at 3 months, makes all IP-related claims uncapped, and grants vendor unilateral 30-day termination; data export exhibit is missing. Produce playbook comparison, clause interactions, minimal draft edits and priority; no law or signature decision.

**Source route:** See golden case in references/eval-suite.json.

**Worked task output**
Clause review draft:

| Priority | Finding | Draft response |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ordinary liability cap is 3 months; supplied playbook calls for 12 months. | Replace “three (3) months” with “twelve (12) months” in the cap sentence, subject to confirming the fee-measurement period and any approved exceptions. |
| 2 | Playbook permits confidentiality/IP carveout categories; contract leaves all IP-related claims uncapped. The record does not define the approved IP subcategory. | Do not call the category itself a deviation. Ask the playbook owner to confirm whether “all IP-related claims” is intended. Draft only: “The foregoing cap does not apply to [approved confidentiality claims] or [approved IP claims, defined by the playbook owner].” |
| 3 | Vendor may terminate unilaterally on 30 days; referenced data-export exhibit is missing. | Request the exhibit. Draft option: “Upon termination, Vendor will make Customer Data available in [format] for [period] and provide [transition assistance] at [approved fee basis].” Bracketed terms are unresolved. |

No governing-law analysis or signature clearance is made.
