# Source applicability and limits

This draft pack adapts methodology from the pinned public sources listed below. Source applicability is not a company-specific legal conclusion and does not establish that any law, standard, certification or framework applies to LiNKtrend. Verify the actual entity, activities, product/data, territories, role, threshold, current version and as-of date from authoritative evidence before making legal or audit conclusions. Unknown is an acceptable status.

- `compliance-framework-gdpr-audit-prep`: GDPR/EEA and possible extraterritorial scope; facts, people/data locations and current authority must be checked

Do not reuse source-era deadlines, thresholds, risk scores, legal conclusions or sample company assumptions without current verification. Voluntary standards, customer requirements and laws must be labeled separately.
