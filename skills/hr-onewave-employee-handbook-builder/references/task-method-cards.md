# Task-specific method cards

Use only the branch matching the requested deliverable. Preserve the full source procedures under `upstream/`; this concise card index is not a substitute for the substantive Phase 2 method.

- **Branch 1:** Interview the owner about actual practices section by section: employment relationship/classifications, pay/timekeeping, leave, attendance, conduct, safety, remote work, benefits, discipline and departures. Record contradictions and unknowns; do not fill them from a generic template.
- **Branch 2:** Review existing handbooks/policies and map each statement to current practice, decision owner and source. Mark conflicting or unenforced language for resolution; do not present aspirational policy as existing practice.
- **Branch 3:** Draft only approved policy decisions in plain English. For each policy, state what it covers, who owns it, the employee-facing rule and how a question/exception is raised. Use realistic examples based on supplied facts.
- **Branch 4:** Include reporting paths for sensitive concerns with more than one owner-provided route. Avoid promising absolute confidentiality or guaranteeing employment outcomes.
- **Branch 5:** For each work location, flag clauses where law may vary (leave, wages/hours, breaks, final pay, privacy, safety, accommodations, restrictive covenants, required notices). Verify current official primary authority or identify counsel review; do not assert an unverified rule.
- **Branch 6:** Cross-check handbook clauses against one another and against actual practice; remove unsupported commitments, determine whether a policy should be omitted or marked for owner decision, and ensure terms and dates are consistent.
- **Branch 7:** Prepare handbook, acknowledgement, short employee quick guide and a counsel punchlist with section references. Keep draft status visible; do not issue, publish or collect signatures.
