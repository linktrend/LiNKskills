# Commercial NDA Review Cards

## Card A — Matter and document map

Record parties, affiliates actually named, contract version/date, disclosing/receiving side by information flow, transaction purpose, negotiation stage and requested outcome. List governing-law, forum and effective-date clauses verbatim with section references. Record amendments and schedules reviewed; identify omitted attachments. Avoid creating matter files or marking a document privileged. Use a confidentiality label only if the user supplies an approved handling rule.

## Card B — Clause inventory and dependency map

Extract defined terms before evaluating operative sections. For each clause, map: actor → must/may/must-not → protected information or activity → time/scope → exception → remedy. Follow defined terms and cross-references. Compare each obligation with its exceptions, duration and remedies. Flag ambiguity, internal inconsistency, one-sided wording, missing mechanism and obligations that are not confidentiality-only. Quote only the minimum necessary passage with page/section.

## Card C — Risk and position analysis

Separate three axes: (1) textual/legal issue supported by jurisdiction-specific authority or expressly labeled as a question; (2) company playbook deviation; (3) business/operational burden. Rate severity and likelihood only with stated reasons; use qualitative high/medium/low or “unrated” when evidence is inadequate. “Playbook silent” means no company position is evidenced. It does not mean accept, reject, market, enforceable or unlawful. Explain plausible counterparty rationale and strongest alternative interpretation for material issues.

## Card D — Drafting / redline options

For each material issue provide preferred clause text and at least one fallback where useful. Use narrowly scoped replacement text; mark unknown party, timing, governing-law or process facts in brackets. Include a short rationale for the edit and negotiation cost. Verify defined terms, numbering, internal references, obligation owner, duration, exceptions and consistency with the remaining agreement. If full clause context is absent, do not claim the redline integrates cleanly; provide a standalone proposed clause and name the missing context.

## Card E — NDA scope traps

Check separately for standstill, exclusivity, non-solicit, non-compete, IP rights, license, assignment, ROFR/MFN, broad arbitration/venue, data protection, security, service, purchase and investment duties. Explain why the term exceeds confidentiality and identify the affected business owner or counsel question. Continue reviewing separable confidentiality terms rather than stopping the entire task.

## Card F — Jurisdiction and source discipline

Identify the actual governing law and any relevant location only from agreement/founder-supplied evidence. Legal rules and enforceability require a current authoritative source for that jurisdiction and date. State source title, issuing authority, publication/update date, pinpoint section and access date. If none is available, provide contract-text analysis, mark law-dependent point unresolved and formulate the research question. Foreign-law source cards remain conditional reference material; never generalize them into universal rules.

## Card G — Output and handoff

Deliver a concise decision brief followed by a clause table: provision; text/location; duty; finding; playbook position; legal/business risk; proposed edit; fallback; owner/reviewer. Close with specific questions and decisions. No “GREEN to sign” category. Use “no material deviations identified against [named playbook]” only when every relevant position was actually supplied and checked. Carlos or external counsel decides final legal position and final text; business owner decides commercial tradeoffs.

## Source-specific variants incorporated

- The commercial review sequence preserves the original Claude-for-Legal sequence: establish deal side, verify playbook and document scope, then screen confidentiality terms and route counsel questions. Its signature-routing and destination/privilege behavior are intentionally not active here.
- The deep Borghei review variant contributes recipient/discloser perspectives, immediate red flags, clause issue log, clause reference modules, and preferred/fallback edit fields. Its scripts and default owner/deadline/risk conventions are not invoked or adopted as company policy.
- The Jamie Tso source is scoped to one-way commercial NDAs. Apply that checklist only when the agreement is actually unilateral; do not apply it to mutual NDAs. Its original text, support modules, and license remain unchanged under `references/upstream/lawve-jamie-tso-nda/`.
- Rapid GREEN/YELLOW/RED screening from other source tasks is handled by the separate `legal-nda-triage` task. This full-review workflow may explain severity but never routes a document directly to signature.
