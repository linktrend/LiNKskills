# Journal entry task-method cards

Use only the requested entry branch. These procedures preserve the Anthropic entry-type distinctions and CrewM8 period-accrual basis/already-booked/reversal fields. They prepare balanced proposals and supporting detail; an authorized owner supplies accounting policy, account mapping and approver matrix. Never infer a threshold or post.

## Common entry record and checks

Record entity, accounting period/basis, source event, affected account labels/codes as provided, line-level debit/credit, currency, department/cost center, calculation, source references, memo, reviewer/owner and reversal flag/date. Compare prior treatment only as a consistency signal, not as authority. Reperform each calculation, detect an existing same-period entry, preserve exceptions and confirm total debits equal credits. If account mapping or policy is missing, retain a descriptive proposed line and mark mapping unresolved; do not invent an account.

## AP/services accrual

Evidence: received goods/services, receipt or contract, invoice/PO when available, period coverage, owner-approved estimation basis and amount already booked/paid. Calculate full basis × period share under the supplied policy/formula, then subtract amount already booked for the same item. Propose Dr expense or owner-designated asset / Cr accrued liability. State whether the owner's policy calls for reversal and its date; compare later invoice to estimate as a follow-up, not an automatic adjustment.

## Prepaid amortization

Evidence: original paid amount, benefit start/end, prior amortization, remaining balance and supplied schedule/policy. Recompute eligible period share (straight-line only if the supplied policy says so), cap at the remaining prepaid balance and flag cancellation/change facts. Propose Dr named expense / Cr prepaid asset for the supported amount.

## Fixed-asset depreciation/amortization

Use the owner-provided fixed-asset register and approved method/useful life/salvage assumptions. Recompute the period charge under that method, tie accumulated depreciation and additions/disposals to the schedule, and list unresolved setup/impairment/disposal questions. Propose Dr depreciation/amortization expense / Cr accumulated depreciation/amortization. Do not choose a tax or book method.

## Payroll/bonus accrual

Use authorized aggregate payroll schedule, period days/working-day convention, approved bonus plan inputs, employer cost schedules and previously booked amounts. Calculate salary/benefit/tax/bonus components separately; do not expose employee-level data in reusable output. PTO or jurisdiction-specific accrual appears only if the applicable owner policy/source says to include it. Propose each expense/liability pairing and identify confidential support references without copying unnecessary personal details.

## Revenue/deferred revenue

Use the contract, owner-approved accounting conclusion/performance schedule and evidence of delivery/performance. Separate (a) release of previously deferred amount, Dr deferred revenue / Cr revenue; (b) earned amount not yet billed, Dr receivable / Cr revenue; and (c) cash/receivable received before earning, Dr cash/receivable / Cr deferred revenue. Show the contract/schedule calculation and unresolved performance/variable-consideration question; route accounting-policy conclusions to the controller.

## Concrete illustration

Owner policy says accrue services received evenly by calendar days and auto-reverse next period. A contract supports 12,400 for the full 31-day month; all services were received; 3,100 was already booked for this contract/month. Period portion = 12,400 × 31/31 = 12,400; proposed incremental entry = 12,400 − 3,100 = 9,300. Draft Dr service expense 9,300 / Cr accrued liability 9,300, cite contract and GL evidence, and note reversal on day 1 of next month per owner policy. This illustration is arithmetic only, not a company policy.
