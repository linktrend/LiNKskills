# Task-specific method cards

Use only the branch matching the requested deliverable. Preserve the full source procedures under `upstream/`; this concise card index is not a substitute for the substantive Phase 2 method.

- **Branch 1:** Start by establishing relevant jurisdictions, covered worker types, entity facts, period and requested compliance question. If any materially affects applicability, mark unknown and continue only unaffected mapping.
- **Branch 2:** For each asserted obligation, locate current primary authority (statute/regulation/official agency material), record exact provision/title, effective/current date, jurisdiction and applicability reasoning. Secondary sources may identify leads but do not support final rule statements.
- **Branch 3:** Map each supported obligation to existing policy/process, owner, evidence and review interval. Distinguish `confirmed applicable`, `potentially applicable`, `not established`, `not applicable with basis`, and `evidence missing`.
- **Branch 4:** Compare evidence to the cited requirement without treating a missing record as proof of violation or a policy document as proof of operation. Record factual gap, affected process and exact question.
- **Branch 5:** Prioritize follow-up by supplied risk criteria; if none, sort by scope and evidence gap without legal severity labels. Escalate interpretation, deadlines, protected rights and remediation decisions to employment counsel/HR owner.
- **Branch 6:** Draft an obligation register or gap report with URLs/section refs, checked date and source quotes only as needed. Do not modify policy, submit filings, certify compliance or communicate externally.
