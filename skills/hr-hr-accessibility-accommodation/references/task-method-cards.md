# Task-specific method cards

Use only the branch matching the requested deliverable. Preserve the full source procedures under `upstream/`; this concise card index is not a substitute for the substantive Phase 2 method.

- **Branch 1:** Determine whether the task is process accessibility design or an individual accommodation request; keep the output to that task and restrict personal details.
- **Branch 2:** Describe the barrier in the environment/process and the relevant work function. Do not ask for diagnosis or infer disability. For an individual case, use only information the authorized process permits.
- **Branch 3:** Review the supplied job/process requirements, existing tools and policy. If current local law is relevant, identify the jurisdiction and verify official primary authority; refer interpretation or entitlement to counsel.
- **Branch 4:** Generate practical options that address the described barrier, including low-cost and alternative configurations. For each, state expected functional effect, feasibility questions, privacy/security considerations, owner and trial/check-in measure.
- **Branch 5:** Do not rank options using stereotypes, promise approval, deny a request, disclose health data, or infer a legal duty. Separate evidence from assumptions and employer constraints.
- **Branch 6:** Draft a neutral conversation checklist and implementation-owner tasks for review, including an appeal/escalation path supplied by policy. Do not send communications or make workplace/system changes.
- **Branch 7:** For hiring accessibility, list accessible application/interview formats and consistent job-related evaluation methods; route structural policy changes to the policy owner.
