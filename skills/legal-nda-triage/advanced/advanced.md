# NDA Triage Method

## Input and decision logic
Capture source document, exact agreement side, counterparty relation, deal purpose, NDA form (mutual/one-way/embedded), deadlines, governing law as written, and playbook source. If side is ambiguous, ask a focused question or label assumptions.

Check that each provision has a section reference and one of: `matches_supplied_position`, `deviates_from_supplied_position`, `playbook_silent`, `not_assessed`. Do not turn no-playbook into universal contract defaults.

## Material checklist
- Definition breadth; marking/oral disclosure mechanics; standard exceptions and evidence burdens.
- Purpose and onward disclosure; representatives/affiliates; responsibility for recipients.
- Standard of care and any security duties that exceed the actual data shared.
- Initial term and survival; trade secrets; retroactive disclosure.
- Return/deletion, retained legal copies, backups and certification.
- Residuals, use/retention rights, warranties, remedies, indemnity or fee shift.
- Governing law, venue, arbitration, notice, assignment and order of precedence.
- Non-confidentiality covenants and hidden commercial obligations.

## Source variants and adaptation
KWP/Lawve checklists share the rapid screen and output route; preserve their term categories but reject playbook-free numeric defaults. Borghei contributes a ten-point screen and scan coverage for hidden IP/license, audit, damages and one-sided duties; its code and scoring cutoffs remain immutable source evidence only. Active routing is descriptive and does not authorize signature or system workflow. Full original trees and notices are in `references/upstream/SOURCE-MANIFEST.json`.
