# HR Process and Evidence Audit Workpaper: task method and acceptance

## Trigger and task edge

Use when preparing a scoped HR process audit, personnel-record sample, policy-control evidence review or factual audit findings draft.

The single-task result is: Audit scope, criteria and population tie-out. Separate adjacent work stays routed to its own task.

## Required evidence

- Audit objective, period, population and accountable audit/HR owner
- Supplied criterion, policy or current primary authority with applicability basis
- Population report/query, record count and source reference
- Authorized sample method and evidence access/privacy scope

## Procedure and branch notes

1. Confirm the requested audit scope, process, period, reporting entity/jurisdiction, objective, authorized reviewer and evidence classification. A request for an audit does not prove any specific legal framework applies.
2. Define the population query, filters, dates, status, record count and relevant totals. Reconcile completeness to the authorized system report or disclose the limitation before drawing sample conclusions.
3. Use only the owner-approved sampling method and size. Preserve ordered population, method and seed if random; label targeted samples as judgmental, not representative.
4. For each item, record criterion, evidence reference/date, observed fact, result, exception, source owner explanation and open question. Separate design adequacy from operating evidence.
5. Use criteria only when policy or current primary authority and jurisdiction/applicability are provided or verified. If not, frame as an evidence gap and refer legal interpretation to counsel.
6. Aggregate exceptions only under approved thresholds. Do not assign legal violation, audit opinion, severity or remediation sufficiency without the authorized criterion and reviewer.
7. Deliver scope, tested items, exceptions, limitations, evidence index and proposed owner follow-up. Do not alter records, certify compliance, or distribute sensitive records.

## Acceptance checks

- Every material input and rule has an evidence reference or is labeled an assumption/open question.
- Calculations show formula, denominator, units, population and source date; arithmetic is independently rechecked.
- Findings distinguish observed facts, attributed statements, hypotheses, assumptions and owner decisions.
- Missing evidence blocks only the conclusion that depends on it; complete unaffected sections.
- Personal data is minimized and any small-group disclosure is suppressed under the supplied privacy rule.
- Drafts contain no unsupported legal, company-policy, approval, employment or fairness conclusion.
- No external effect, message, system mutation, filing, signature or business decision occurs.

## Jurisdiction, evidence and escalation

Do not assume a jurisdiction, entity, employment classification, policy, reporting standard or legal duty. For current law, verify official primary authorities for the actual location/date and route interpretation to qualified counsel. Treat secondary articles and source examples as research leads only. Preserve date, record ID, source scope and contradictory evidence. Escalate only material safety, legal, retaliation, privacy or decision-right issues; routine drafts continue within scope.
