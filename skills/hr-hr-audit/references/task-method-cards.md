# Task-specific method cards

Use only the branch matching the requested deliverable. Preserve the full source procedures under `upstream/`; this concise card index is not a substitute for the substantive Phase 2 method.

- **Branch 1:** Confirm the requested audit scope, process, period, reporting entity/jurisdiction, objective, authorized reviewer and evidence classification. A request for an audit does not prove any specific legal framework applies.
- **Branch 2:** Define the population query, filters, dates, status, record count and relevant totals. Reconcile completeness to the authorized system report or disclose the limitation before drawing sample conclusions.
- **Branch 3:** Use only the owner-approved sampling method and size. Preserve ordered population, method and seed if random; label targeted samples as judgmental, not representative.
- **Branch 4:** For each item, record criterion, evidence reference/date, observed fact, result, exception, source owner explanation and open question. Separate design adequacy from operating evidence.
- **Branch 5:** Use criteria only when policy or current primary authority and jurisdiction/applicability are provided or verified. If not, frame as an evidence gap and refer legal interpretation to counsel.
- **Branch 6:** Aggregate exceptions only under approved thresholds. Do not assign legal violation, audit opinion, severity or remediation sufficiency without the authorized criterion and reviewer.
- **Branch 7:** Deliver scope, tested items, exceptions, limitations, evidence index and proposed owner follow-up. Do not alter records, certify compliance, or distribute sensitive records.
