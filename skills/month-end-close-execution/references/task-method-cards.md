# Month-end close execution method cards

Use this skill to prepare and track a period close package. It does not perform source-system writes or establish completion. The full pinned CrewM8 monthly-close original is preserved under `references/upstream/gokulsvision--crewm8-cfo-skills@c814ff97743e/skills/reporting-compliance/monthly-close-process/SKILL.md`.

## Card A — Close checklist phases

Organize evidence by entity-configured phase: transaction cutoff/subledger tieouts; revenue and deferred-revenue schedule preparation; accrual and balance-sheet rollforwards; trial balance/variance review; financial-statement handoff. Treat close calendar, assigned owners and due dates as source facts only when supplied. Preserve checklist row IDs and state transitions. A deadline does not authorize skipping a reconciliation or representing it as complete.

## Card B — Core source tieouts

For bank/card, AR, AP and payroll: compare the dated statement/register/subledger total to the corresponding GL balance for same entity, period, currency and basis. Record source totals, scope, unmatched rows, calculated difference and owner. For deferred revenue, compare the ledger liability to the approved schedule's remaining unrecognized amounts. For fixed assets/prepaids, compare register/schedule rollforward to GL. Each tieout is its own result; a break is not fixed by this close orchestration task.

## Card C — Accrual and rollforward drafts

Prepare calculation drafts only with supported accounting treatment and inputs. Examples of source methods to route to the appropriate dedicated task: unbilled AP from services received and contract/time support; payroll accrual using eligible earned days and supplied pay basis; prepaid amortization from coverage period and approved method; depreciation from approved cost/life/method; commission/bonus from contract/policy; bad-debt allowance from approved aging/rate policy. Formulas in the original are examples, not universal policy. Cite every amount and ask accountant/controller when method, cutoff, estimate or policy is not supplied. Mark all proposed entries unapproved/unposted.

## Card D — Revenue and deferred revenue review

Inventory active contracts and source schedules. Separate billed cash, invoiced amounts, earned activity, usage/service-delivery evidence, and deferred balances. Apply only the supplied approved recognition policy/accountant conclusion. Recompute schedule totals and compare with GL; record missing contract, usage, or delivery evidence. Do not decide recognition timing from contract value alone and do not post adjustments.

## Card E — Review, variance and handoff

Compare to prior period or budget only when comparable data exists; show absolute delta and percentage with formula/denominator and source periods. Leave thresholds and materiality to approved company policy. Compile evidence refs, prepared schedules, unreconciled breaks, assumptions, reviewer, next owner, and due date. Only a source record showing review/approval/posting/closed state supports those labels. Financial statement preparation, audit evidence packet, and close-calendar ownership remain separately routed task outputs.
