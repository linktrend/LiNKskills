# Profile Change Cards

For every domain, identify the exact setting, existing value, proposed value, evidence, owner, effective date, dependent skill methods, migration impact, approval, and review date. Do not treat a newly proposed rule as active before owner decision.

- **AI governance:** use-case registry, AI vendor review, impact-assessment approach, prohibited uses, risk owner.
- **Commercial contracts:** sales/purchasing posture, playbook position, NDA preferences, escalation route, review output.
- **Corporate:** active module, board/consent format, deal profile, materiality or disclosure schedule preferences.
- **Employment:** worker/work-location scope, review trigger, handbook position, hiring/termination workflow.
- **IP:** portfolio scope, brand/clearance/enforcement preference, assignment/open-source review.
- **Litigation:** firm role, party side, risk vocabulary, house style, case intake.
- **Privacy:** controller/processor position, DPA/DSAR/PIA workflow, incident route, policy commitments.
- **Product:** launch review gates, claim substantiation, risk calibration and escalation.
- **Regulatory:** monitored authorities, policy library, materiality decisions, source/update cadence.

The nine immutable source cards and exact licenses/notices are in `references/upstream/`. Their config-writing commands are not active. Only apply a change via an expressly supported owner interface after required approval; otherwise provide the proposal and interface gap.
