# Macro regimes, scenarios, and bubble-risk successor proposal v0.1.1

Status: isolated source-completion proposal for owner review; not canonical or behavior-qualified. Scope: approved group 6, “Macro regimes, discretionary global macro, scenarios and bubbles.” This integrates structural regime hypotheses, event-driven scenarios, and a separate bubble-risk evidence ledger. It does not adopt any pinned repository’s composite score, regime threshold, bubble score, probability band, fixed allocation or action band. Those sources define materially different signals and some depend on provider-fetch scripts. The method accepts user-supplied or otherwise owner-authorized data only.

## Typed input contract

Analysis request: as-of timestamp/timezone; country/economy; horizon (`structural_months_to_years`, `tactical_weeks`, or `event_scenario`); requested branches; assets/holdings/exposures and constraints if portfolio advice requested; scenario/event identity if relevant. Every numeric series observation requires instrument/series ID, publisher, source pointer, observation date/time and timezone, publication/vintage timestamp, numeric value, unit, currency if monetary, frequency, transformation/version, adjustment convention, and coverage start/end. For ratios, specify numerator and denominator series, units, source and aligned timestamp; for correlations, state return basis, frequency, lookback and minimum paired observations. For yields, units must state percent or basis points; spreads preserve subtraction order and both legs. For earnings/inflation/employment observations, identify actual/estimate/previous, period, seasonal adjustment and preliminary/final vintage. For valuation, name measure, numerator/denominator, basis and units. For policy analysis, provide policy setting, meeting/calendar identity, prior communication, observation/release date, market-implied expectations and source vintage when those comparisons are requested. Holdings, NAV, available cash and risk constraints are optional and must each carry currency/as-of and source.

Missing/late/revised/discordant inputs are valid states, never coerced to zero. Mark each factor `available`, `partial`, `unavailable`, `stale`, `not_comparable`, or `not_requested`, with reason and source pointers. A monthly structural branch requires enough dated observations for its stated comparisons; daily tactical evidence cannot silently stand in for monthly macro data. No source fetching, ETF universe, correlation calculation, or live access is implied.

## Output contract

Return independent output sections with `complete_from_supplied_evidence`, `partial_named_missing_items`, or `unavailable_missing_basis` status:

1. **Structural regime hypothesis:** horizon/as-of, named supported regime descriptions, component observations and comparisons, evidence for/against each hypothesis, lag/coverage, competing regime and “what would change this view.” Classifications are hypotheses, not established labels or probabilities.
2. **Scenario set:** baseline and material alternative scenarios; for each, causal chain as a hypothesis, initiating conditions, observable signposts, directional asset/sector exposures only if mapped to supplied instruments/data, counterevidence, time horizon and invalidation conditions. Probabilities only when a documented, calibrated, same-target and same-horizon forecast set exists; otherwise use unweighted qualitative cases.
3. **Bubble-risk ledger:** distinct factor-family rows (valuation/fundamentals; leverage/financing; issuance/speculation; participation/breadth; volatility/positioning/sentiment; narrative/market structure), each with the actual supplied measure, observation/vintage, interpretation, contradicting evidence, missing required inputs and source. The conclusion is “evidence raises/does not raise a specified concern” or “insufficient evidence”; it is not a scored bubble diagnosis.
4. **Portfolio implication/proposal:** only if holdings, NAV, currency, constraints and instrument exposures are supplied. State scenario sensitivity and an advisory target/risk or increase/trim/exit proposal with evidence and uncertainty; distinguish proposal from authorization/execution. No holdings means no personalized allocation target.
5. **Sources, calculations and limitations:** units/signs, date alignment, transformations, data versions, missingness, historical-vintage limitations and non-causal interpretation. Never imply forecasts were backtested or calibrated when they were not.

Any material/live/capital change requires Lisa and Carlos independent approval channels for the exact version. The analysis does not submit an order, sign, activate a strategy or mutate policy.

## Integrated method

1. **Separate horizons and claims.** Classify the request before reading indicators: structural macro regimes (monthly, roughly 1–2 year question); tactical market breadth/top evidence (daily/weekly); or event-conditioned scenarios. Present each on its own basis. Market-top evidence is not a macro regime; an event scenario is not a regime forecast. If multiple horizons are requested, provide separate rows and do not average their conclusions.

2. **Check source vintages and comparability.** For each observation preserve period end, release date, vintage/publication time, frequency, units and transformation. Use only facts available as of the analysis timestamp for a historical “what was knowable then” comparison. A revised macro series is not substituted into the original vintage. Match calendar/session and align monthly ratios at the same month-end; retain missing months. If two inputs have different frequency, show the conversion rule and its limitation or do not compare. Currency-convert only with a specified rate/source/time and do not mix nominal and real measures without labeling.

3. **Build per-component structural evidence (do not source-score).** The selected source family names six useful distinct cross-asset observations: equal-weight/market-cap concentration (RSP/SPY), yield curve (10Y−2Y), credit (HYG/LQD), size (IWM/SPY), equity/bond relationship (SPY/TLT and/or correlation), and cyclical/defensive rotation (XLY/XLP). They are candidate observations, not mandatory tickers or validated weights. For a ratio, compute numerator level / denominator level only when same-date, same-currency, consistently adjusted values exist; it is dimensionless only if both operands share units. For a period return from adjusted level `P`, calculate `r_t = P_t/P_(t−1) − 1`, with dates and adjustment basis stated. For a yield spread, state `10Y yield − 2Y yield` and basis points or percentage points; positive/negative interpretation depends on units and country. For correlation, use aligned same-frequency return pairs and a single consistent sample convention: `rho_xy = cov(x,y)/(sd(x) × sd(y))`; report return definition, paired observation count, lookback, and convention. It is dimensionless in [−1,1], not evidence of causal linkage; insufficient paired observations or nonstationary periods make it unavailable/descriptive. Report actual observed direction and alternative explanation, not generic regime scores.

   For a broader global-macro dashboard, group supplied observations into growth/activity (GDP, PMI, employment), inflation (CPI/PCE and expectations), liquidity, housing, consumption, sentiment, central-bank policy/reaction function, meeting calendar, nominal/real yield curves, market-implied rate expectations, FX, commodities, credit, volatility and financial conditions. Preserve country, seasonal adjustment, frequency, vintage and units; “risk-on/off” is an interpretation of aligned evidence, not a raw datum. Real yield is nominal yield minus same-country/tenor/maturity-matched inflation breakeven; swap spread is swap rate minus a same-currency/tenor government yield, reported in basis points. If those legs, source terms, or matched dates are missing, do not compute. Cross-market indices, FX, commodities and volatility belong in a separate dated dashboard and do not replace monthly structural observations. Web search, macro-market MCP data, or a vendor terminal is not assumed available.

4. **Compare trend without lookahead.** If supplied monthly series has adequate history, show its current value against explicitly requested moving averages or prior-window values. An SMA of monthly observations is the arithmetic mean of the last N completed month-end values; label N and month-end convention. A 3-month/12-month change is `(x_t/x_(t−k))-1` only for positive comparable level series; for a spread crossing zero, report point/basis-point change rather than a ratio percentage. Crossover claim requires both averages and prior/current comparison values; no future bars or revised future vintages. Missing history makes that component unavailable, not neutral. The source’s 6/12-month SMA and ROC layers can inform a transparent comparison if requested, but their score weights, proximity cutoffs, recency points, transition probability ranges and classification decision tree are not adopted.

5. **Form a regime hypothesis with a countercase.** Summarize which observations are consistent with concentration/broadening, contraction, inflationary/cross-asset stress, or transitional/mixed conditions, using plain-language definitions and citing each input. Give the strongest alternative classification and evidence against the leading hypothesis. If signals conflict, data vintages disagree, or components are absent, say “mixed/insufficient” rather than force one of the source’s five labels. Historical analogs are descriptive context only; they cannot establish the current state or a probability.

6. **Build event or macro scenarios.** For an identified event/headline, record known fact, source/time, affected economy/market and what is unknown. Classify the event (policy/rates, geopolitics, regulation, technology/industry, commodity/supply, corporate/M&A) and trace first-order direct effects, second-order value-chain/financial effects, and third-order feedbacks only where each link has evidence or is explicitly marked as a hypothesis. Develop a baseline plus bull/upside and bear/downside alternatives when the user requests a three-case set, as explicit chains (`condition → possible transmission → observable market/economic signpost`), e.g. inflation persistence → rate expectations → duration/credit sensitivity, but only claim exposure if actual instruments/data are supplied. If an 18-month view is requested, separate 0–6, 6–12 and 12–18 month conditions rather than precision forecasts; otherwise honor the specified horizon. Each scenario states what observation would support or falsify it, counterpart explanations and horizon. A second-opinion check, if an independent reviewer actually exists, should examine omitted effects, consistency, optimism/pessimism, alternative scenarios and timeline; never invent a subagent review. Do not attach probabilities merely from narrative completeness. If supplied forecasts are calibrated, document target, forecast date, horizon, resolved outcomes, calibration sample and scoring rule before reporting them.

7. **Assess bubble risk through independent factor families.** Preserve separate evidence, not a formula: (a) valuation relative to sourced cash-flow/earnings/growth basis and history; (b) leverage/margin/debt/financing conditions; (c) issuance/IPO/speculative financing; (d) breadth/concentration and market participation; (e) volatility, positioning, options, put/call or sentiment measures with exact definitions and vintage; and (f) observable narrative/behavioral evidence. For each factor, specify the measure, denominator, lookback and evidence threshold used only as a description—not an adopted cutoff. Record supportive and contradictory data and whether the factor is missing. Source bubble frameworks name these factor families, while alternate sources use incompatible scoring and stage rules; do not compute/quote source score or label “bubble” from one factor. If core evidence is unavailable or mixed, conclude “insufficient evidence for a bubble-risk classification.” Distinguish expensive valuation, speculative excess and imminent crash; they are different claims.

   **Optional crypto-only branch:** when crypto is explicitly in scope and the user supplies authorized, timestamped series, report BTC trend structure, alt participation, BTC dominance, funding/leverage, drawdown/volatility and short-horizon breadth as separate rows with universe/source/vintage. Do not import CoinGecko/Binance endpoints, a moving top-N universe, local accumulating cache, weighted score, missing-weight redistribution, or risk-on/off bands from the quarantined crypto analyzer. No data/API access is implied. Missing history (especially dominance history) means that factor is unavailable; it must not be backfilled or treated as neutral. Keep crypto findings separate from equity macro regime; only synthesize when a supplied portfolio exposure makes that relevant.

8. **Translate to advisory portfolio implications only when contract permits.** Map each scenario to supplied holdings, instrument currency and exposure; state the directional sensitivity and risk pathway, with bounds or scenario values only when data support them. A recommendation may propose increase, trim, exit, target weights or risk levels if the user asks and portfolio/risk inputs are sufficient. Show proposed change as advisory and identify the assumption that would reverse it. Missing NAV, holdings, contract multiplier, FX, duration/beta/sensitivity or constraints leaves that branch partial/unavailable. Do not issue orders, enable automation or change policy. Use existing LiNKtrading system; no alternate broker installation.

9. **Review accuracy and deliver.** Recalculate units, signs, frequencies, period alignment, denominator, missing/stale flags and source pointers. Explicitly identify if a result is descriptive, scenario analysis, or calibrated forecast. Ensure no future revision leaks into as-of analysis; no source score/probability is presented as Jane’s. Show date, horizon, named regime hypotheses, scenario comparison, bubble ledger, countercase, portfolio proposal if supported, and next evidence that would change the view.

## Discriminating cases

- 10Y=4.20% and 2Y=4.55%: spread is −0.35 percentage points (−35 bps), not +35; show leg order/units.
- Numerator ETF close is split-adjusted but denominator is not: ratio unavailable until adjustment conventions align.
- Monthly values contain a missing month and one revised release: preserve both missingness and vintage; do not backfill or use revised future value in historical as-of comparison.
- A yield spread crosses zero: report point change, not percentage rate-of-change with unstable denominator.
- Six listed source components have only two supplied: report those two and four unavailable; no source-weighted composite score or low-risk conclusion.
- RSP/SPY broadens while credit tightens and defensive rotation rises: state mixed evidence and countercase; do not force broadening/contraction score.
- High valuation multiple with no cash-flow/growth basis: valuation context incomplete; do not label bubble.
- Bubble source score is high but leverage/issuance/breadth inputs are missing: reject source score as unsubstantiated; ledger shows missing factors.
- Headline-only scenario and no calibrated resolved forecast sample: qualitative scenario only; no probability.
- Portfolio request with no holdings/NAV or instrument sensitivity: no target/allocation; provide general scenario map only.
