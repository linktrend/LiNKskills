# Synthetic successful performance management system design

## Request

Synthetic design: 12 managers, 48 employees, quarterly cycle. Propose 4 quarterly stages and a review form with 3 measurable role goals, employee self-reflection, manager evidence and development action. Do not impose a rating distribution or tie draft rating to pay.

## Method

Apply the active task procedure. Preserve the stated evidence references, units, assumptions and owner boundaries.

## Expected artifact

Performance cycle design and annual/quarterly calendar with assumptions, source refs, and any owner decisions clearly separated.
