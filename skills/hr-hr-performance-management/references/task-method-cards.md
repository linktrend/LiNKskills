# Task-specific method cards

Use only the branch matching the requested deliverable. Preserve the full source procedures under `upstream/`; this concise card index is not a substitute for the substantive Phase 2 method.

- **Branch 1:** Define whether the work is system design, process improvement or an individual employee review; route individual review completion separately.
- **Branch 2:** Map the cycle stages from goal setting through check-ins, evaluation, calibration, development and follow-up. Name outputs, role owners, timing and dependencies using supplied company facts.
- **Branch 3:** Design observable goal criteria that connect role outcomes to measures, evidence sources, timeframe and controllability. Avoid forced distributions, universal rating scales or performance targets unless approved policy supplies them.
- **Branch 4:** Draft a review form/process with self-reflection, manager evidence, development needs and employee response. Separate feedback/development from compensation decisions unless owner policy defines a transparent linkage.
- **Branch 5:** Design calibration using role-relevant evidence, consistent definitions and documented variance questions. Treat outlier distributions as prompts to inspect criteria/data, not proof of bias or poor performance; do not force a curve.
- **Branch 6:** Add manager training and employee communication outlines, accommodations/accessibility review and a process for questions/appeals. Do not send messages or apply ratings.
- **Branch 7:** Define process measures (completion, quality, timeliness, goal clarity, feedback usefulness) and privacy-safe review. Present proposed cycle, test cases and decisions for owner approval.
