# Performance Management System Design

**Owner:** Sara for preparation and private drafting; the accountable HR, business, finance, privacy or counsel owner retains decisions. **Lifecycle:** draft, uncertified, not admitted.

For the `lisa-openclaw` consumer, use native agent SQLite/session checkpoints. Treat the portable `persistence.state_path` (including `.workdir/tasks/...`) as contract text only; never create sidecar files from it.

## Scope

Use when designing or improving the organization’s performance-management cycle, forms, goal-setting standards, manager enablement or calibration process. Individual review execution is a separate task.

**Inputs:**
- Cycle objective, roles/populations and timeframe
- Current review/goal/calibration documents and supplied policy
- Manager/employee feedback themes, response coverage and approved evaluation principles
- Decision owners and dependencies with compensation, promotion or performance systems

**Expected outputs:**
- Performance cycle design and annual/quarterly calendar
- Goal/review/calibration guidance and manager support tools
- Measurement and fairness checks with open design choices

**Scope boundary:** routine authorized read and private drafting are allowed within visible tool scope. The skills prepare evidence, calculations, checklists and options only; they do not execute HR or business-system actions. Do not require separate founder approval for harmless private drafts. Ask only for facts that materially change the requested output.

## Phase 2 — Task method

1. Define whether the work is system design, process improvement or an individual employee review; route individual review completion separately.
2. Map the cycle stages from goal setting through check-ins, evaluation, calibration, development and follow-up. Name outputs, role owners, timing and dependencies using supplied company facts.
3. Design observable goal criteria that connect role outcomes to measures, evidence sources, timeframe and controllability. Avoid forced distributions, universal rating scales or performance targets unless approved policy supplies them.
4. Draft a review form/process with self-reflection, manager evidence, development needs and employee response. Separate feedback/development from compensation decisions unless owner policy defines a transparent linkage.
5. Design calibration using role-relevant evidence, consistent definitions and documented variance questions. Treat outlier distributions as prompts to inspect criteria/data, not proof of bias or poor performance; do not force a curve.
6. Add manager training and employee communication outlines, accommodations/accessibility review and a process for questions/appeals. Do not send messages or apply ratings.
7. Define process measures (completion, quality, timeliness, goal clarity, feedback usefulness) and privacy-safe review. Present proposed cycle, test cases and decisions for owner approval.
