# Domain Bootstrap Cards

## Common interview frame
Operating model, parties/users, work jurisdictions as evidenced, domain scopes, owners/authority, approved workflow, sources, current tools, escalation rules, output artifacts, retention/privacy limits and open decisions. Distinguish factual answer, founder preference, existing policy, legal requirement, draft proposal and unknown.

## AI governance
Builder/deployer roles, AI use-case inventory, data categories, risk owner, prohibited uses, vendor review and impact-assessment style. Legal applicability requires current source verification.

## Commercial contracts
Sales vs purchasing, agreement types, negotiation objectives and fallback positions, clause playbook, escalation routes, NDA triage expectations and review output.

## Corporate
Entity/equity/board/secretary/public-company/deal modules selected only from founder statements and source records. Capture which events need minutes/consents, decision authority and existing records; never infer entity or authority facts.

## Employment
Employer entities, worker types/locations as evidenced, handbook and contracts, hiring/performance/termination workflows and escalation owners. Do not invent jurisdiction table or employment rules.

## IP
Asset types, ownership/assignment evidence, clearance/enforcement posture, open-source review, filing deadlines and outside-counsel routes. Never infer title.

## Litigation
Role (in-house, firm associate, solo) and party-side posture only after explicit founder response; matter intake, evidence handling, deadline and escalation practices. Avoid matter details not needed for profile.

## Privacy/data protection
Controller/processor roles per activity, personal-data categories, data subject request, DPIA/PIA and DPA workflow, incident owners and source policies. Do not set response deadlines without authority.

## Product counsel
Launch review process, decision stages, claims substantiation, risk calibration and escalation. Any materiality category must be grounded in founder decision and past evidence.

## Regulatory
Watchlist, source library, materiality decisions, review owner and update cadence. Jurisdictions and feeds remain founder-provided and verified; no universal threshold.

All original domain interview packs are copied unchanged with repo license under `references/upstream/<domain>/`. They guide the cards but their local commands/config writes/integration probes are not executable here.
