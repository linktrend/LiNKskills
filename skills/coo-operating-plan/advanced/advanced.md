# Coo Operating Plan: detailed procedure and variants

1. Read approved company direction and current executive plans; classify objectives as approved, proposed or unresolved.
2. Translate the specified horizon into outcomes, measures, milestone dates, accountable domain owners and dependency handoffs; do not become a second CEO or take over Program Ledgers.
3. Compare workload with agent/human/vendor/provider capacity and budget evidence; identify conflicts and uncertainty.
4. Define review cadence, escalation, exceptions, continuity and measures of accepted outcomes rather than activity counts; use subsidiary task skills for detailed mapping/procurement/change work.
5. Return the integrated plan and decision package to Sara/Lisa. Material priorities/spend remain proposals until the authorized approval; track implementation and effectiveness separately.

## Source comparison

Both are COO advisory/operating-plan methods. Merge strategic alignment and execution cadence; preserve detailed subsidiary workflows as separate skills.

## Failure recovery

On missing evidence, preserve known answers and isolate the affected conclusion. On conflicting evidence, record both source dates and ask the accountable owner to reconcile. On tool denial, use an authorized Sara-mediated extract or report operation/owner/error; never bypass the boundary. On a failed draft write, inspect the actual write result and readback before retry to avoid duplicates. On an unavailable specialist, retain the assignment and dependencies; Sara may do bounded work within her role but cannot invent the specialist review.
