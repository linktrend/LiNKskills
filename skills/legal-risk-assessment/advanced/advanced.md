# Risk Assessment Cards

## Severity and likelihood
Assess dimensions separately before combining. Severity is the consequence if the defined event occurs, not the intensity of concern. Likelihood is probability over a stated horizon, not a confidence score. Record evidence and counterevidence for each. If company rubric is provided, quote its label and version and calculate only as that rubric specifies. Without one, give an uncalibrated qualitative range; do not present numeric multiplication as validated.

## Risk register entry
Fields: stable risk ID; concise event; category; affected objective; evidence and date; scenario/trigger; severity; likelihood/horizon; current control and test evidence; exposure; mitigation; residual risk; owner; decision authority; next review date; escalation question; status. Preserve history through the authorized owner interface rather than adding local runtime state.

## Escalation logic
Surface urgent deadlines, credible severe harm, active dispute/investigation, regulator contact, potential personal data/safety exposure, and uncertain legal authority as concrete reasons for timely human/counsel attention. These are review prompts, not universal legal mandates. Do not select or engage counsel or make a final reporting decision.

## Source variants
KWP and Lawve use identical severity-by-likelihood memo and register structure. Borghei contributes periodic register review, risk scoring and escalation workflows. Keep rating factors explainable; do not import its monetary thresholds or mandatory escalation trigger as company policy. Original guides/scripts are retained under `references/upstream/` and are not executed.

## Integrated jurisprudence verification improvement

When a conclusion depends on case law, retrieve current primary authorities for the actual jurisdiction and search for contrary, narrowed or overruled decisions, not only supporting cases. Use recent commentary as a discovery aid and verify its propositions against primary text. Record decision dates, subsequent treatment, conflicting authority and verification gaps before rating risk; age alone neither invalidates nor proves a rule. Cite verified authorities inline with source/date and distinguish unsupported recollection. GoodLegal/French/EU examples in the copied variant apply only if the matter and currently granted tool support them; do not invent those tools or universal age thresholds.
