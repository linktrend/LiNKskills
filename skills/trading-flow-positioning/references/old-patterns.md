# Source audit repair rules

- 13F is delayed, disclosed long-position data; never infer short exposure or current holdings.
- Keep COT contract category, report date, publication date, lookback, and open-interest denominator distinct.
- Do not combine conflicting source reliability-grade formulae or arbitrary threshold bands.
- Unknown access, incomplete coverage, unmatched identifiers, short history, or zero range must remain explicit.
- COT crowding alone is not an entry recommendation.
