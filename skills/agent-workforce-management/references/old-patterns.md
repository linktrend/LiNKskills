# Old patterns blacklist

- Do not treat a role description as an active agent or runtime identity.
- Do not copy credentials, tokens, cookies, account bindings, or private memory
  into a role pack, fixture, telemetry record, or subordinate prompt.
- Do not author, approve, activate, or enforce a Brain rule from a workforce
  review.
- Do not turn a capability request into a technical grant or an authority grant.
- Do not silently convert delegation into a scheduled task, message, or mutation.
- Do not claim suspension or retirement occurred when only a proposal exists.
- Do not use repeated failure as permission to punish, delete, or disable an agent.
- Do not infer completion from missing, stale, or unreported evidence.
