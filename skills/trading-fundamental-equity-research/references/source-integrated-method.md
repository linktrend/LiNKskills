Inputs: issuer identity and listing/jurisdiction; research as-of timestamp and information cutoff; user’s research question and intended horizon; filings and other supplied or explicitly accessible primary sources with publication time, accession/document identity, page/section, currency, units, fiscal period, and source rights; historical statements or user-approved source access; market price/share count/date; peer set with comparability evidence if provided; and output/tool availability. Outputs: (1) dated company/industry evidence brief, (2) normalized historical statement schedule and auditable forward model when inputs suffice, (3) valuation methods and conditional range when assumptions/data suffice, (4) charts only for supported series, and (5) assembled coverage report with source notes, countercase, sensitivity, limitations, and uncompleted-deliverable ledger. Return each file/artifact actually produced with path/type/hash and a coverage manifest. ## Integrated five-stage method

1. **Company research — primary source: initiating-coverage/SKILL.md task 1; support: task1-company-research.md.** Establish business model, segments/products, customers, suppliers, management, competitive setting, capital structure, material risks, and reported milestones. For every numeric fact record the reported period, filing/publication date, currency, unit, and source location. Keep issuer statements, third-party estimates, and analyst inference separate. Reconcile contradictions across amended filings and restatements; preserve both source dates. Do not fill a management biography, customer concentration, TAM, market share, or ownership claim without sourced evidence. Output: source-linked company brief plus open questions.

2. **Financial model — primary source task 2; support: task2-financial-modeling.md.** First establish a statement map for income, cash flow, and balance-sheet line items across reported periods; normalize units and fiscal periods without erasing original values. Document recasts, acquisitions, FX conversion, segment restatements, and classifications. Project only drivers with explicit evidence or stated analyst assumptions, label historical/estimate, and maintain formula lineage. Provide revenue/segment or geography schedules only if filings actually disclose them. Reconcile cash flow, balance sheet, shares, and statement totals; flag rather than silently plug imbalances. Create a dynamic workbook only when an available approved spreadsheet tool can write/read back formulas and values. Workbook content: historical schedules, assumptions, projection statements, scenario cases, and valuation inputs; add a check sheet or equivalent audit output. The upstream six-tab workbook and row-count quotas are source design details, not Jane requirements.

3. **Valuation — primary source task 3; support: task3-valuation.md and valuation-methodologies.md.** Select a method because it fits issuer economics and available data: DCF where cash flows and discount/terminal assumptions are defensible; trading comparables where peer economics/accounting/periods/market dates can be normalized; asset or other methods only where the business supports them. For a DCF with consistent currency and annual forecast periods, unlevered FCF_t = EBIT_t × (1 − tax_rate_t) + D&A_t − CapEx_t − change_in_NWC_t; tax rate is dimensionless, and the monetary terms must use the same currency/period. Discounted FCF_t = FCF_t / (1 + WACC)^t. A perpetuity-growth terminal value is FCF_(n+1)/(WACC − g), only when its assumptions are explicit and WACC is greater than g; otherwise that method is undefined and must not be presented. State units, as-of price, share class, diluted share count, debt/cash/leases/minority interests, FX basis, forecast periods, tax, discount rate, terminal method, and sensitivities. Bridge enterprise value to equity value and per-share value; show method-specific limits and disagreement across methods. Any price target/range and BUY/HOLD/SELL wording is advisory and conditional on sourced inputs and the user's applicable governance; do not invent a rating or imply an order. Enterprise-to-equity bridge uses one currency/as-of basis and explicit signs: equity value = enterprise value + cash and non-operating assets − debt − preferred claims − non-controlling interests (with lease liabilities treated consistently in both EV and cash flows); per-share value = equity value / diluted shares. If denominator, FX, period, current price, or essential driver is unavailable, return the range as unavailable and name the blocking input.

4. **Charts — primary source task 4; support: task4-chart-generation.md.** Build a chart only from an identified source series or a fully reproducible calculation already in the model. For every chart retain series definition, units, universe, period, transformations, cut-off, and supporting rows. Prioritize decision-relevant figures such as historical/projected revenue, margins/cash flows, scenario comparisons, or valuation sensitivity, according to actual available data. Omit a chart whose underlying segment, price, peer, or valuation input is absent; do not draw placeholders or interpolate missing observations as fact. Upstream chart count, fixed chart numbering, resolution, and benchmark vendor names are not mandatory. Use a charting tool only if actually present and permitted; otherwise supply a chart specification and mark it not generated.

5. **Coverage report assembly — primary source task 5; support: task5-report-assembly.md.** Synthesize the company brief, normalized model, valuation, and supported charts into an internally consistent report. Lead with thesis and countercase; distinguish reported facts, estimates, calculations, and judgment. Every figure/table has a source and as-of or a clearly marked analyst assumption. Reconcile all reported values to workbook/calculation outputs. Include investment thesis, risks, business/industry, historical results, forecast assumptions, scenario analysis, valuation methods/range, catalyst context, and data gaps to the extent evidence exists. Do not inherit the upstream fixed 30–50 page length, word count, chart/table quota, copied text, or mandated BUY/HOLD/SELL rubric. If DOCX generation is unavailable, deliver a structured report in an actually supported format and disclose the file-format limitation.
