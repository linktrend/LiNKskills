# Task-specific method cards

Use only the branch matching the requested deliverable. Preserve the full source procedures under `upstream/`; this concise card index is not a substitute for the substantive Phase 2 method.

- **Branch 1:** Confirm the requisition is approved/budgeted from an authorized record. If status cannot be verified, draft planning options but do not represent the role as approved.
- **Branch 2:** Translate role outcomes into observable must-have criteria and separately labeled preferences. Remove criteria that are unsupported, vague or unrelated to the work; do not infer protected traits or legal rules.
- **Branch 3:** Build a sourced role description with responsibilities, level, requirements, location and supplied pay/benefit terms. Mark missing facts and route policy/legal wording to the correct owner.
- **Branch 4:** Choose sourcing channels and target stage definitions based on the role and evidence supplied. State time-to-fill, conversion and cost formulas with cohort, dates and denominators; omit unsupported external benchmarks.
- **Branch 5:** Design structured screening and interview steps. Use consistent competencies/questions and independent evidence notes; candidate evaluation scorecards are produced by `hr-onewave-hiring-scorecard` when that deliverable is requested.
- **Branch 6:** Analyze stage movement only from a consistent application cohort. Show counts entering/leaving each stage, conversion, time-in-stage and missing records; do not attribute disparity or quality differences to protected groups without qualified, lawful analysis.
- **Branch 7:** Provide decision-support comparison or candidate packets only when authorized, job-related criteria and evidence exist. Do not rank by protected traits, make hiring decisions, or infer from resume gaps.
- **Branch 8:** Prepare offer and new-hire handoff details as private drafts, routed to offer drafting/onboarding skills. Never contact candidates or mutate ATS.
