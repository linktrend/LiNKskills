# Recruiting Process Design and Coordination: task method and acceptance

## Trigger and task edge

Use for end-to-end recruiting process planning for an approved role: role definition, sourcing design, structured screening/interview plan, funnel review and handoff. Use the separate pipeline skill for status-only tracking.

The single-task result is: Role profile/job-description draft or recruiting plan. Separate adjacent work stays routed to its own task.

## Required evidence

- Approved requisition reference, role, level and hiring owner
- Must-have qualifications, desirable criteria, location and budget constraints
- Current applicant funnel data or sourcing evidence, if analysis requested
- Hiring stages, interview panel, decision criteria and authorized communication owner

## Procedure and branch notes

1. Confirm the requisition is approved/budgeted from an authorized record. If status cannot be verified, draft planning options but do not represent the role as approved.
2. Translate role outcomes into observable must-have criteria and separately labeled preferences. Remove criteria that are unsupported, vague or unrelated to the work; do not infer protected traits or legal rules.
3. Build a sourced role description with responsibilities, level, requirements, location and supplied pay/benefit terms. Mark missing facts and route policy/legal wording to the correct owner.
4. Choose sourcing channels and target stage definitions based on the role and evidence supplied. State time-to-fill, conversion and cost formulas with cohort, dates and denominators; omit unsupported external benchmarks.
5. Design structured screening and interview steps. Use consistent competencies/questions and independent evidence notes; candidate evaluation scorecards are produced by `hr-onewave-hiring-scorecard` when that deliverable is requested.
6. Analyze stage movement only from a consistent application cohort. Show counts entering/leaving each stage, conversion, time-in-stage and missing records; do not attribute disparity or quality differences to protected groups without qualified, lawful analysis.
7. Provide decision-support comparison or candidate packets only when authorized, job-related criteria and evidence exist. Do not rank by protected traits, make hiring decisions, or infer from resume gaps.
8. Prepare offer and new-hire handoff details as private drafts, routed to offer drafting/onboarding skills. Never contact candidates or mutate ATS.

## Acceptance checks

- Every material input and rule has an evidence reference or is labeled an assumption/open question.
- Calculations show formula, denominator, units, population and source date; arithmetic is independently rechecked.
- Findings distinguish observed facts, attributed statements, hypotheses, assumptions and owner decisions.
- Missing evidence blocks only the conclusion that depends on it; complete unaffected sections.
- Personal data is minimized and any small-group disclosure is suppressed under the supplied privacy rule.
- Drafts contain no unsupported legal, company-policy, approval, employment or fairness conclusion.
- No external effect, message, system mutation, filing, signature or business decision occurs.

## Jurisdiction, evidence and escalation

Do not assume a jurisdiction, entity, employment classification, policy, reporting standard or legal duty. For current law, verify official primary authorities for the actual location/date and route interpretation to qualified counsel. Treat secondary articles and source examples as research leads only. Preserve date, record ID, source scope and contradictory evidence. Escalate only material safety, legal, retaliation, privacy or decision-right issues; routine drafts continue within scope.
