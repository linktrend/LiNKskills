## Pre-interview working examples — not legal forms or legal advice

- Board minutes: the authored neutral blank outline is available at `references/reference-register/entries/template-083/INDEX.md`. It is a working example, not a legally sufficient form. Confirm entity type, governing documents, jurisdiction, notice, quorum, voting, conflicts, approval, and record-retention requirements with counsel before use.
- Written consent: the authored neutral blank record is available at `references/reference-register/entries/template-084/INDEX.md`. It is an administrative outline, not a valid consent form by itself. Counsel or the responsible owner must confirm the eligible body and signers, governing provision, delivery, threshold, and effective-date rules.
- Consultant services and work-product example: the captured Common Paper Professional Services Agreement is at `references/reference-register/entries/template-074/INDEX.md`; the standalone Statement of Work example is at `references/reference-register/entries/template-101/INDEX.md`. The PSA provides example provisions for deliverables, pre-existing and third-party materials, and assignment timing. These are source examples, not company-approved terms; confirm current edition, jurisdiction, and applicability with the human owner and counsel. The PSA source states CC BY 4.0 attribution requirements.
- Cooley GO exact-source leads: the US consulting agreement page is `references/reference-register/entries/knowledge-045/INDEX.md`; the employee confidentiality and inventions assignment page is `references/reference-register/entries/knowledge-046/INDEX.md`; the document library is `references/reference-register/entries/knowledge-047/INDEX.md`. The maintained pages route to interactive generators. The consulting page describes a US-company form; Cooley describes its CIIAA as for US companies with California employees. The generated form text was not captured through ordinary public retrieval, so keep both named forms as source gaps. Determine worker type and relevant work locations after interview, then have the human owner/counsel decide whether either route applies. Do not treat the employee CIIAA as a consultant form.
