View the Standard Terms About this Common Paper agreement What is the AI Addendum? The AI Addendum is a supplementary document that is added to a contract to address the use of artificial intelligence (AI) in a specific product. The AI Addendum outlines terms specific to the use of AI technologies, such as data usage, intellectual property, training rights, liability, and ethical considerations. It helps clarify how AI will be treated within the scope of the contract and ensures that both parties are aligned on key concerns related to AI. When should I use the AI Addendum? The AI Addendum is designed to be used whenever an agreement involves the use of AI technologies, whether it’s for AI-based services, AI-driven products, or the incorporation of AI into business processes. If the contract involves AI-generated content, automated decision-making, or the use of data for AI model training, the AI Addendum ensures that all AI-specific terms are clearly outlined and understood. Does the AI Addendum require an underlying agreement? Yes, the AI Addendum is meant to be used alongside another agreement. It can be added as an amendment to an existing agreement that has already been signed or included as part of the original agreement for a new or in-progress deal. What types of agreements can I use the AI Addendum with? The AI Addendum can be used with the Common Paper CSA or Software License Agreement . It is designed to be a modular add-on to Common Paper standards, and the defined terms and variables may not be correct if it is used with a non-Common Paper agreement. How does the AI Addendum affect the underlying agreement? The AI Addendum is a separate document that modifies or supplements the terms of the primary agreement. It clarifies specific aspects of the contract related to AI use, but it does not replace the main agreement. Both documents should be read together to fully understand the rights, responsibilities, and expectations of both parties. The addendum should explicitly reference the primary agreement and be incorporated by reference to make it part of the contractual obligations. Why are there different versions of the AI Addendum? There are a few different ways to use the AI Addendum, and each one has its own version. To add the AI Addendum to an existing, already signed agreement, use the Standalone Amendment version. To attach the AI Addendum to an in-progress agreement in the Common Paper platform, use the In-App Attachment version. To use the AI Addendum for a new deal outside the Common Paper platform, use the Cloud Service Agreement (with AI) or Software License Agreement (with AI). How do I use this AI Addendum? Finally, sign the Order Form with your counterparty via the signing process of your choice. Can I customize the AI Addendum? Yes, you can feel free to change the AI Addendum any way you like by using the Cover Page. The only thing you are required to keep is the license information and link to the Standard Terms. I see this agreement is hosted online. Does that mean it will change? The latest version of the Addendum is 1.0, and it will remain unchanged and hosted at commonpaper.com/standards/ai-addendum/1.0 . We expect future changes to occur infrequently, and they will be posted as a new version when they happen. New versions will not change or otherwise impact agreements that incorporate prior versions. Can I customize the Standard Terms? All modifications to the Standard Terms should be made on the Cover Page. Incorporating the Standard Terms by reference from the Common Paper website gives both sides assurance that all key details and modifications are explicitly called out in the Cover Page.
