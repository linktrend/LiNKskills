# Employee Invention and Work Product — Neutral Intake Outline

It is not the Cooley GO form, not copied from a legal form, not legal advice, not an employment agreement, and not an assignment or promise by any person or company. No terms are adopted by completing it. Use it to collect facts and route questions to qualified counsel after the actual worker, work scope, and relevant work locations are known. Do not request confidential material belonging to a current or former employer.

## 5. Scope questions for counsel — no terms selected

For each topic, record the question, facts needed, reviewer, source, and disposition. Do not turn a blank or broad clause into a company decision.

- How should covered work be defined by duties, resources, time, and relationship to the employer’s actual business?
- How should inventions or work conceived before, during, or after the relationship be treated, and what exclusions or disclosures are required?
- What written schedule or disclosure process is appropriate for pre-existing materials, and how should later additions be handled?
- What assignment, license, consent, or no-transfer treatment is appropriate for approved pre-existing/third-party material, if any?
- What confidentiality rules and exceptions apply, including required disclosures or protected activity?
- What assistance/cooperation after the relationship ends may be requested, with what expense, notice, limits, and authority?
- What work-location-specific statutory restrictions, exclusions, notices, timing requirements, or non-waivable terms apply? Counsel must identify the relevant law and current source; do not assume a waiver or use a generic rule.
- Which governing-law, dispute, remedies, signature, delivery, and recordkeeping terms need professional drafting/review?

## 7. Safe completion criteria for this intake

The intake is ready for counsel only when the work scope, worker relationship as a fact (without legal classification), every relevant work location and date, known pre-existing items, third-party materials, sources, unknowns, and owners are recorded. Unresolved legal applicability remains open. This outline itself does not authorize access, use, assignment, hiring, policy adoption, or signature.
