 In this representation, the Company confirms that it is not a “covered person” subject to restrictions on handling U.S. data under the new DOJ Data Security Program, often colloquially called “data export” rules.  The second sentence indicates that the Company has complied with the DSP since it took effect in April 2025. Adding the third, bracketed sentence of the representation may be appropriate when the investor believes the company likely possesses covered datasets.  The additional language will allow an investor to ensure the Company has established policies and procedures for DSP compliance, to ensure that it has addressed the (complex) compliance requirements for data holders under the DSP.
The investors’ right of recovery for a breach of this rep may be broader than under SEC Rule 10b-5, because in order to prevail in a Rule 10b-5 securities fraud action, the purchaser must establish that the seller acted with scienter. That is, a purely innocent misrepresentation normally does not give rise to civil liability under 10b-5.
