The information on this page applies to Basic View and Professional View users of the FASB
Accounting Standards Codification® (ASC).

AAA users should consult the FAQ at https://aaahq.org/Research/FASB-GARS for additional
information.

If your question is not listed here, or if you require further assistance, please contact FASB
Support at fasbpubs@fasb.org.

Basic View Users

Why can't I use certain features of the Codification?
While all Codification content is the same in both Basic View and Professional View,
advanced features are only available with Professional View. To view the list of Professional
View features and functionality, or to access those additional features by purchasing the
Professional View, please go to the FASB Store.

How can I leave feedback?
After entering the Basic View, scroll down the main window of the home page to the Help
section and select the “General Feedback” link. Select the type of feedback, enter your
comment, and click Submit. If your feedback refers to a specific paragraph, include the
paragraph number in your comment.

Where can I find the Other Sources documents?
Electronic versions of Accounting Standards Updates, Proposed Accounting Standards
Updates, Other Exposure Documents, Pre-Codification Standards (Superseded Standards),
and Concepts Statements are available in the STANDARDS and REFERENCE LIBRARY
sections of the FASB website.
The most recent Maintenance Updates are available in the ASC. After entering the Basic
View, scroll down the main window of the home page to the What’s New section.

What are the differences between the Basic View and the Professional View of the
Codification?
Basic View is free and may be accessed without creating an account or logging in. Features
include:
   •   Topically organized access to all authoritative nongovernmental U.S. GAAP, including
       relevant SEC content.
   •   Utility to identify the location of original standards. A cross-reference report may be
       generated, but the links in the resulting report will NOT be active. The Cross-
       Reference report may be found by accessing the Tools link, towards the bottom of
       the left navigation pane, then selecting “Cross-Reference & Sourcing Tool.”)

How do I purchase a Professional View subscription?
To purchase a Professional View subscription:
   1. Go the FASB Store.
   2. Select FASB Codification Online from the navigation menu on the top of the screen.
   3. Scroll to # of concurrent users in shopping cart on upper right of screen (single user
      to 20 concurrent users) and “add to cart.” Please contact customer support at
      fasbpubs@fasb.org if you would like to purchase a subscription for more than 20
      concurrent users.
   4. Click Login/Register if you are not already logged in with your account.
   5. Enter email address and password or “sign up now” to register your account.
   6. Complete checkout process, including address information.
   7. Enter participants (name and email address).
   8. Review order.
   9. Accept the Licensee agreement terms and conditions.
   10. Enter and submit your credit card information.
   11. Confirm your order.
   12. You will receive a registration confirmation email including a verification code and
       password setup process.

Professional View Users

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
To reset your password:
   1. Go to the FASB Store and click Login, then click Forgot Your Password? It will ask for
      your email address to send a verification code.
   2. Enter the verification code received in the email and click Verify code.
   3. Enter and confirm your new password and click Continue.

I know my password, but I would like to change it. How do I change my
password?
Log in to your account on the FASB Store and go to Manage My Account/Login Credentials.
It will ask for your email to send a verification code to. Enter the verification code received
in the email and click “Verify code.” Enter and confirm your new password and click
Continue.

How do I change the email address/email domain name/username?
Your email address is your username and allows you to access the Codification. If you are
the account administrator, please contact FASB Support at fasbpubs@fasb.org. If you are a
participant (you did not directly purchase the subscription), please contact your account
administrator.

Where can I find my account information?
Log in to the FASB Store and click Manage under My Account. On this page you can edit
addresses, payment information, manage participants (if account administrator), and
change other profile information.

How do I find my account administrator?
Log in to the Codification with your Professional View credentials and click the My ASC link
located towards the bottom of the left-hand navigation pane. On the resulting page, click
the My Subscription Information link. Your account administrator will be listed towards the
bottom of the resulting page.

I closed my browser window without logging out of the Codification. What should
I do?
Assuming your system enabled cookies, you should be able to reopen the screen and click
Logout at the bottom of the left navigation pane. If not, you may log back in and then out of
the system to ensure you have properly logged out. If you require further assistance,
please contact FASB Support at fasbpubs@fasb.org.

Professional View Administrators

How do I renew my subscription?
If you are an account administrator, you may process renewals:
   1. Go to the FASB Store.
   2. If not logged in, enter your username and password.
   3. Click on Manage under My Account on the bottom of the screen. Click on
      Subscriptions under View My Activity and you should see your Current Subscription
      and its Renew button (Note: Renewals are available starting 90 days before the
      expiration date).
   4. Click the Renew button beneath the appropriate subscription.
   5.   Follow any additional instructions and then check out.

How do I change (add, remove, edit) participant information in my account?
If you are an account administrator:
   1. Log into the FASB Store.
   2. Under My Account, click Manage.
   3. Under View My Activity, click Subscriptions.
   4. Manage participant information (add/remove/edit).
   5. For a new participant, enter the new user's name and email address. The Participant
      will receive an email with login instructions.

I am NOT an account administrator and am receiving an error message that says,
"You have already reached the maximum number of concurrent user sessions
allowed by your subscription." What should I do?
Your account administrator has purchased a defined number of seats. If you are not an
account administrator, please contact your account administrator so they can determine if
they wish to purchase additional seats, or you may wait for a seat to become available once
a current user logs out.

I am an account administrator. My account's users are seeing an error message
that says, "You have already reached the maximum number of concurrent user
sessions allowed by your subscription." What should I do?
If you are an account administrator, you may either purchase additional seats to reduce the
likelihood of reaching maximum numbers of seats allowed, or, if you believe that no
additional seats are needed, you may manage the number of current seats by accessing
https://asc.fasb.org. You will see a message that says, "You have reached the maximum
number of concurrent user sessions allowed by your subscription." Beneath that message,
you will see a list of users currently logged in. Click Log Out beside the session(s) you would
like to drop.
OR, as the account administrator, you can leverage the “Current Activity” page from the My
Account>My Subscription link in the lower section of the left navigation pane in order to see
a list of users already using the application with a Logout button in front of their names.
Click Log out to drop the users.

How many users can log in to my account at one time?
For each concurrent user license you purchase for your account, you may register up to nine
(9) additional users. For example, if you purchase a five-user license, you may register an
additional forty-five (45) individuals who can access the product. (In this example, only ten
users would be able to log in and use the product simultaneously.)
If you need to register more than the maximum number of users your license allows, you
can either remove some of the existing users from your account or purchase an additional
license. You can find the number of concurrent user seats for your account clicking My
Account tab.

Can I cancel my account?
Subscriptions to the FASB Codification are not refundable. Your account will be available
through the end of the current term, and will terminate at the expiration date.
