## PMI public charter article

- Source: PMI, Narendra K. Shrivastava, “Do More in Less: Deliver Projects Successfully with Only Eight Powerful Project Management Processes,” presented at PMI Global Congress 2014 — North America; page dated 2014-10-26: https://www.pmi.org/learning/library/eight-project-management-processes-9362
- Classification: official PMI-hosted conference paper/article, readable without a login; it is dated 2014 and describes a proposed practical approach, not current PMBOK 8 or a universal requirement.
- Readable source facts: Article describes a project charter as an authorization/alignment artifact. It says the charter is collaborative and can record sponsor and team, business outcome, duration and measurable goals, scope and deliverables, milestones/dates, issues/risks, success factors, assumptions/constraints, high-level budget/schedule/contingency, and approvals.
- Short source excerpt: “project begins with a clear vision that is mutually agreed upon.”
- Interview-use checklist: ask who can sponsor/authorize work; what outcome is sought and how it will be measured; what belongs in and out of scope; what deliverables and acceptance evidence exist; who must contribute or approve; what dependencies, milestones, assumptions, constraints, risks, timing, and capacity are known; and whether any approval has actually occurred. Record unknowns as unknowns. The checklist is an interview aid, not authorization to start work or to set a budget.
- Route test: article body and charter field list were readable in the public web reader; no login gate appeared on the article page. The full article is not copied here.

## PMI KICKOFF public page

- Source: PMI, KICKOFF: https://www.pmi.org/kickoff/ (public landing page); course start route: https://kickoff.pmi.org/
- Classification: official public course description, not a standard or PMBOK text. PMI page describes the course as free and under an hour, offers Agile or Predictive tracks, downloadable templates, and stage-based project questions.
- Short source excerpt: “free course developed by PMI.”
- Useful scope: KICKOFF is a possible introductory learning route for a human operator. Public landing-page information can support neutral interview prompts; it does not establish that a company uses PMI methods.

I did not register, log in, enroll, or claim that course lessons/templates were retrieved. Whether the interactive course requires an account remains unverified.
