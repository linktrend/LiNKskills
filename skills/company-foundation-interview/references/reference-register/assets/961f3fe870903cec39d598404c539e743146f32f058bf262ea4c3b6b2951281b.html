Six Sigma Team Charter Template

Download a Blank Six Sigma Team Charter Template for
Microsoft Word  | Google Docs

When to Use This Template: Use this template to formalize Six Sigma projects to ensure you clearly define project goals, scope, roles, and metrics to drive process improvements and quality management.

Notable Template Features: This template includes detailed sections for problem statement, Six Sigma roles (e.g. Champion, Master Black Belt, Black Belt, Green Belt, and team members), business case, DMAIC phases and deliverables, and key metrics. This dynamic Six Sigma-specific template provides a structured approach to identifying performance gaps, setting measurable goals, and outlining the steps and metrics for achieving Six Sigma quality improvements.
