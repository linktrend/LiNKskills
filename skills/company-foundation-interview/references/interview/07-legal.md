# Legal matters, contracts and corporate records

Owner: Legal/corporate specialist.

Why: Matter-specific facts, dates and jurisdiction determine legal research and review needs.

## Known context to prefill

- Carlos and external counsel review relevant legal work; templates are starting points, not governing authority.

## Read records before questions

- minimized matter/contract and corporate record index
- representative agreements/IP/privacy records
- deadline/source and counsel-review evidence

## Adaptive unanswered questions

- Which corporate records, contracts, IP rights, licenses and confidential obligations already exist?
- Which privacy/data roles, customer/vendor locations and contractual terms apply?
- Are there disputes, claims, evidence-preservation needs or known deadlines?
- Which drafting/redline/playbook/signature standards and external counsel contacts are approved?

## Branches and exceptions

- Foreign-law template: preserve procedural usefulness but research actual jurisdiction separately.
- Deadline without source: flag urgently for verification, do not invent rule/date.
- Confidential matter: restrict audience, preserve evidence, no external counsel/vendor messages during intake.

## Expected preparation outputs

- matter/contract/obligation register
- legal research and drafting requirements
- professional review/escalation package
