# Existing books, reporting and tax preparation

Owner: Controller and tax/compliance specialist.

Why: Opening balances, account design and reporting must reflect existing records and chosen treatment.

## Known context to prefill

- Self-hosted Odoo Community is intended; default company configuration is not a completed accounting setup.
- Accounting method, chart of accounts and tax treatment remain unresolved until facts and approvals exist.

## Read records before questions

- trial balance/subledgers and opening schedules
- prior financials/tax returns and accountant decisions
- reporting examples and fiscal calendar

## Adaptive unanswered questions

- Where are existing books, assets, liabilities, equity and opening-balance support?
- Who prepares/reviews accounting and tax work, and what decisions are already documented?
- What close/reporting period, comparison, budget and management information does the Principal need?
- Which prior or upcoming filings, deadlines and supporting records are known?

## Branches and exceptions

- Empty Odoo data: report unconfigured/empty state, not no business activity.
- Conflicting balances: isolate reconciling questions rather than choose one source.
- Missing basis/elections: keep COA and policy draft neutral and route decisions for review.

## Expected preparation outputs

- existing-record/opening-balance assessment
- reporting/tax/close requirements
- COA/policy decision and review inventory
