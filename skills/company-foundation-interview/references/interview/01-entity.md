# Entity, ownership and jurisdiction

Owner: Legal specialist with tax/compliance.

Why: These facts determine which official requirements apply; an incorporation address does not establish tax election or worker location.

## Known context to prefill

- Wyoming incorporation: Principal-reported in Sara Setup; request existing formation records before repeating question.
- Miami registered/main office: Principal-reported; not proof of operating or work location.

## Read records before questions

- formation and ownership documents
- tax election/filing evidence
- registration/address/work-location records

## Adaptive unanswered questions

- What entity type, exact legal name, ownership and fiscal year do the existing formation and tax records establish?
- What tax elections or prior filings exist, with effective dates and preparer?
- Where does the company actually conduct business and where does each human/contractor work?
- Which state/local registrations and registered-agent arrangements already exist?

## Branches and exceptions

- Conflicting legal names or elections: preserve both sources and ask a targeted reconciliation question.
- No filing history supplied: mark unknown, not no filings.
- Foreign work/business location: identify local applicability research and professional-review dependency, not a US-only conclusion.

## Expected preparation outputs

- entity/location fact register
- jurisdiction/election questions
- corporate/tax obligation research assignments
