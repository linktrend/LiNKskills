# Banks, funding and financial authority

Owner: Treasury specialist.

Why: Cash visibility, reconciliation and approval depend on known accounts and delegated authority.

## Known context to prefill

- No bank authority or payment limit is established by Sara's CFO role.

## Read records before questions

- minimized account inventory and statements
- funding/loan records
- current payment authority evidence

## Adaptive unanswered questions

- Which authorized bank/card/processor accounts, balances and statement periods are available?
- What funding, loans, owner contributions/distributions or capital commitments exist?
- What approved payment/signature limits and evidence of authorizations already apply?
- What information may Sara receive from Jane's trading/custody operations without taking over execution?

## Branches and exceptions

- Missing statement balance/date: do not infer cash or reconciliation success.
- No documented payment authority: prepare authority questions; do not execute payments.
- Trading/private principal account: clarify company scope and permission before access.

## Expected preparation outputs

- cash/account inventory
- funding and authority gaps
- reconciliation/forecast and approval requirements
