# Human employees and consultants

Owner: Finance Operations/human HR specialist.

Why: Worker location, relationship and terms drive HR/payroll requirements; small size is not a blanket exemption.

## Known context to prefill

- One human currently; potentially no more than five humans plus consultants: Principal-reported.
- HR consultant and applicable professional review are part of the review chain.

## Read records before questions

- minimized workforce/consultant and location register
- approved agreements/classification evidence
- payroll/provider/policy records

## Adaptive unanswered questions

- Who is engaged by which entity, in what work location, with which existing agreement and duties?
- What evidence supports employee/contractor classification and actual working arrangements?
- What compensation/payroll/benefit/leave/timekeeping processes and providers exist?
- Which onboarding/offboarding, access, conduct, accommodations, complaints or performance procedures are required?

## Branches and exceptions

- Founder versus worker role: do not infer payroll or exemption.
- Conflicting location or classification: request actual arrangement evidence and current official guidance.
- Sensitive complaint/health records: restrict audience and ask only necessary facts.

## Expected preparation outputs

- human/consultant applicability intake
- HR/policy/payroll requirement register
- HR consultant review package
