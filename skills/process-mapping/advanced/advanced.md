## Integrated task controls

1. Define SIPOC boundaries, trigger, terminal result, unit of work, variant, process owner and observations. Observe actual execution before the workshop; prefer timestamps and artifacts over recalled durations.
2. Map the current path by responsible role/system in swimlanes, with inputs, outputs, decisions, queues, handoffs, re-keying, exceptions and rework return points. Validate it with people who perform the work; do not design the future path as if it were observed.
3. Measure touch time, wait time and rework separately from compatible samples. Report sample period/count and stage distributions, then compare the map against actual end-to-end cases. Never sum stage percentiles and call that an end-to-end percentile.
4. Inspect handoff density, owner changes, waiting share, system switches, duplicate entry, ping-pong and rework. Identify the constraint and causal uncertainty; source thresholds and Little Law apply only with their stated assumptions, not as universal company rules.
5. Prioritize remove, consolidate, parallelize and standardize before automation. Quantify labor savings from touch time only; monetize elapsed-time benefit only with owner-supported evidence. Record future-state proposals, dependencies, risks and acceptance measurements separately.
