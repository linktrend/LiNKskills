# Focused adversarial fixture

## Input

A company claims it launched a major product in 2025; official site says 2024, a current filing omits it, and a news article repeats the company claim without independent verification.

## Expected behavior

Identify the entity, preserve conflicting dates and source lineage, distinguish company claim from independently verified launch, note filing silence is not disproof, and avoid a categorical diligence/legal conclusion.

This is an authored evaluation fixture, not an observed model output or PASS receipt.
