# CFO advisory task cards: intake and output-specific analysis

These cards enrich the strategic decision-preparation base with the useful Borghei intake discipline. They do not import fixed benchmarks, universal CFO lifecycle assumptions or the source's asserted runway/freshness thresholds.

## Common intake and baseline card

1. Identify the requested deliverable (options memo, forecast/model, unit-economics analysis, fundraising scenario or board package), its audience, decision date, period, entity/currency, accountable decision owner and success criterion. If the user already supplied these, proceed without repeating questions.
2. Use authorized records and supplied assumptions to assemble a dated baseline. Record each source's as-of date and the baseline period. Compare source age to an owner-supplied freshness rule if one exists; otherwise report exact dates and do not label data current/stale using an invented cutoff.
3. Ask only for missing audience, deliverable, baseline or decision facts that would materially change the work. Continue unaffected calculations with labeled gaps.
4. Return an output matched to the audience, with facts, derived calculations, assumptions, hypotheses and owner choices distinguished. Route detailed accounting, tax, legal or operational tasks to their named skills.

## Options memo card

Build a table of options, one-time and recurring impacts, cash timing, key assumptions, downside case, alternative explanation and decision criteria. Reconcile option amounts to the dated baseline; do not call a scenario a forecast unless its assumptions and owner status are clear. State the decision owner and unresolved inputs.

## Cash/runway card

Show cash at the as-of date, monthly net cash use over the stated period, simple runway calculation (cash ÷ monthly net cash use only when that measure is meaningful), and a period-by-period scenario when seasonality or planned changes make the ratio misleading. Include downside drivers and the cash date/period. Do not apply source-defined universal runway bands.

## Unit economics card

Define the cohort, period and numerator/denominator for each metric. Recompute contribution, acquisition cost and payback only from supplied, consistently scoped data. Show small denominators, incomplete cohort maturity and attribution uncertainty; no upstream industry benchmark becomes a company target.

## Fundraising/board card

List proposed capital amount/use, timing, stated terms or owner assumptions, cash impact and sensitivities. Preserve term-sheet values as provided; route legal interpretation to counsel. For board materials, present trend, variance, outlook, material risks and exact decisions requested, with each chart tied to the same dated data baseline.
