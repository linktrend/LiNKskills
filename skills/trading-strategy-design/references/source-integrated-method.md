# Trading thesis, hypothesis, edge design, and strategy comparison: successor proposal v0.1.1

Status: isolated source-completion proposal for owner review, not canonical, enabled, or qualified. The approved group 0 explicitly includes strategy comparison. The frozen package has a useful single-hypothesis experiment ticket, but its input/output does not require multiple alternatives or produce a comparison table. This proposal adds that missing research branch while retaining explicit separation between advisory research and implementation/execution.

## Task input and output

Inputs: user question, objective and horizon; one or more candidate strategies; eligible instrument/universe, side, holding period, signal/entry and exit definitions, risk/invalidation concept; causal mechanism and expected participants; point-in-time data fields/timestamps/granularity and coverage; venue/session/corporate-action conventions; user-provided constraints; benchmark and comparison criterion; known fees/spread/slippage/borrow/funding assumptions with units/source; and whether results exist. Missing assumptions stay null and limit conclusions.

Output: hypothesis register (one record per candidate), comparable experiment plan, common evidence/cost/risk/data contract, falsification and kill criteria, explicit pairwise or ranked comparison only when user weights/criteria permit, decision label (research-only, rejected, needs evidence, or ready for Eric to scope implementation), and remaining inputs. When more than one candidate exists, compare them side-by-side with identical horizon, universe, data cutoff, benchmark, trade construction, costs and validation split. Do not manufacture quantitative winner or edge when measures are unavailable; mark non-comparable dimensions and state what evidence would discriminate them.

## Integrated method

1. **State candidate hypotheses.** For each strategy state instrument/universe, direction, mechanism, why the market may not already price it, holding period, entry/exit/invalidator, risk thesis, required features and expected failure conditions. Separate descriptive pattern from causal edge hypothesis. If user provides a mechanism but no executable rule, record the unknown trigger rather than invent one.

2. **Classify evidence.** Trace each item to reproducible trade logs, a specified backtest output, structured journal observation or anecdote. Use those categories as qualitative evidence provenance, not numeric source weights. Separate regime-specific observations from broadly asserted effects. Record conflicting evidence and a falsifiable countercondition for every candidate. Unsupported feature frequency, data granularity, fills or survivorship assumptions are blocking evidence, not inferable defaults.

3. **Normalize the comparison.** Build a candidate-by-criterion matrix. Required rows: mechanism, target universe, point-in-time availability, implementation/data complexity, turnover/liquidity, tail/short risk, capital/funding, estimated friction, robustness evidence, falsifiers and unresolved items. Use common units/timeframes and clearly mark incomparable candidates. A weighted total is allowed only if Carlos/user supplied weights or approved criteria exist; otherwise compare as a qualitative Pareto table and expose the tradeoffs.

4. **Write a discriminating experiment.** Define the event/decision timestamp, information available then, label/target and horizon, train/validation/test boundaries, baseline and ablation, missingness, corporate actions, universe membership, transaction-cost model, capacity/liquidity and short borrow/funding assumptions. Prevent lookahead, label leakage, survivorship, repeated holdout tuning and selection bias. For any supplied results, distinguish gross and net returns: net P&L in currency = gross P&L − commissions/fees − spread/slippage − borrow/funding and other stated costs; do not combine units or apply a cost more than once. Express return only against a stated notional/capital denominator and period. Predeclare success/failure criteria and number of trials. If historical data/results are not supplied or authorized, emit a prospective test plan and do not label it passed.

5. **Prepare an engineering handoff where relevant.** Describe the exact data fields, signal semantics, order-independent position/risk proposal, expected outputs and failure cases for Eric to map to the existing LiNKtrading contracts. Identify when a strategy is research-only versus needs engineering. Do not produce/submit orders, connect a broker, run an upstream script, add an alternate broker, or change live state.

6. **Report advice and governance.** Jane may recommend a candidate or propose increase/trim/exit, targets and risk levels when holdings, fees, data and mandate inputs justify it. Label each recommendation advisory with assumptions and invalidation. Material/live/capital action requires Lisa and Carlos independent approvals through two channels for the exact version. Sara owns legal/accounting. No signing, activation or policy mutation.
