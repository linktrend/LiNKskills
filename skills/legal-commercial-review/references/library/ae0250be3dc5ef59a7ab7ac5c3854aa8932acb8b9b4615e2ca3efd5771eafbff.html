Meet the Committee

A streamlined structure for commercial contracts

Common Paper agreements have two parts:

THE COVER PAGE

Contains the variables that allow you to customize an agreement to suit your specific needs. All negotiations happen on the cover page.

THE STANDARD TERMS

The static structure that creates reliability. Standard terms are practical for everyone.

Standards make contracting easier for everyone

Our standard agreements are drafted to start each negotiation on fair and reasonable terms for both sides. Because the Standard Terms never change, both vendors and buyers can review theirs once and rely on them over and over.
