# Superseded reporting patterns

- Hardcoded 06:00/08:00 collection schedules instead of consuming a supplied
  window.
- Treating every reported item as completed.
- Mixing Routine calendar events, Principal Tasks, and deadlines.
- Reading or summarizing a mailbox outside the owner's scope.
- Full agent transcripts, health/selfie content, or redundant battery requests.
- Empty headings, emojis by default, transport claims, or completion without
  evidence.
