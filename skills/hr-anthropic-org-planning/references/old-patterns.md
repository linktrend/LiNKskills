# Old patterns and failure guards

- Do not infer policy/legal rules, market pay benchmarks, review ratings, or hiring status. Require cited source or label the item an assumption.
- Do not treat source connector names as actual Sara tools; use visible current native schemas.
- Do not turn routine draft preparation into an approval gate. Reserve owner decisions for consequential HR outcomes.
- Do not send or mutate HRIS/ATS/policy systems as part of analysis or draft writing.
- Do not expose identifiable sensitive data in aggregate outputs.
