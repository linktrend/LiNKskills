# Tax source-document organizer: method cards

This is an evidence collection/indexing task, not tax advice. The Anthropic preparer-packet flow is the base; OneWave adds a W-9/1099 document-completeness checklist. No jurisdiction, filing duty, threshold, form eligibility or deadline is inferred. Use tax-specific branches only after the owner or accountant identifies the applicable entity, period, jurisdiction and requested document set; if not supplied, create a general records inventory and mark applicability unknown.

## Common accountant-packet card

1. Record confirmed entity/legal name, tax year/period, jurisdictions explicitly supplied, requested preparer deliverable and who supplied each fact. Unknown and not applicable are different statuses.
2. Create one row per requested evidence item: source category, expected period, custodian, received/missing/not-applicable status, file/reference ID, date, coverage, duplicate/conflict, confidentiality class and follow-up owner.
3. Reconcile packet totals to provided closed-book reports/control totals where requested; identify gaps without changing books or deriving a tax liability.
4. Return an indexed private packet manifest, missing-items list and explicit unresolved applicability questions for the accountant. Do not transmit or file externally.

## Conditional U.S. W-9 / 1099 document-request card

Activate only when the requester/accountant confirms a U.S. tax-year vendor-information or 1099-preparation packet is in scope. For each vendor on the owner-provided population, index the vendor identifier/name, supplied payment-source period and total, W-9 requested/received/date/reference, payment detail received/missing, duplicate or name/TIN mismatch flag if visible, and accountant-review status. Compare payment totals across the provided ledger/payment processor extracts and document the exact covered sources and period. Do not apply a dollar threshold, decide reportability, determine recipient classification, validate a TIN against external systems, or conclude a form is required; hand those questions to the accountant using current primary authority. A missing W-9 is a document gap, not a legal conclusion.
