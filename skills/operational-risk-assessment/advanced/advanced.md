# Operational Risk Assessment: detailed procedure and variants

1. Scope the service/process, stakeholders, objectives, critical dependencies and material assets.
2. Describe risk as event, cause hypothesis and impact, with evidence/source and uncertainty; separate actual incidents from possible future events.
3. Assess likelihood and impact using a declared scale and rationale, not fabricated probabilities; inspect controls and distinguish designed from tested-effective.
4. Compare avoid/reduce/transfer/accept options, cost, ownership, dependencies and residual uncertainty. Risk acceptance belongs to the authorized owner.
5. Produce review triggers, continuity and escalation references; track mitigation completion separately from demonstrated effectiveness.

## Failure recovery

On missing evidence, preserve known answers and isolate the affected conclusion. On conflicting evidence, record both source dates and ask the accountable owner to reconcile. On tool denial, use an authorized Sara-mediated extract or report operation/owner/error; never bypass the boundary. On a failed draft write, inspect the actual write result and readback before retry to avoid duplicates. On an unavailable specialist, retain the assignment and dependencies; Sara may do bounded work within her role but cannot invent the specialist review.
