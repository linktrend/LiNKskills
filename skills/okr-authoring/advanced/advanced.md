# Okr Authoring: detailed procedure and variants

1. Confirm strategic intent, scope, horizon and accountable domain owner; no objective becomes policy merely because a template suggests it.
2. Draft outcome-focused objectives and key results with units, baseline, target, deadline, source and measurement owner; missing baselines remain unknown.
3. Separate initiatives/activities from outcomes and show dependencies, capacity and cost implications.
4. Stress-test gaming incentives, controllability, contradictory goals and overlap with other executives; do not prescribe a universal ratio of committed/aspirational goals.
5. Return an approval-ready scorecard and review method. Changes follow approved planning feedback; do not fabricate scores or force an arbitrary document length.

## Failure recovery

On missing evidence, preserve known answers and isolate the affected conclusion. On conflicting evidence, record both source dates and ask the accountable owner to reconcile. On tool denial, use an authorized Sara-mediated extract or report operation/owner/error; never bypass the boundary. On a failed draft write, inspect the actual write result and readback before retry to avoid duplicates. On an unavailable specialist, retain the assignment and dependencies; Sara may do bounded work within her role but cannot invent the specialist review.
