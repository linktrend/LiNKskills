# Financial statement preparation cards

Use the card that matches the requested statement and the available post-close records. The pinned Anthropic `financial-statements` and CrewM8 `financial-statement-generation` originals remain under `references/upstream/`; their GAAP citations/examples are not current legal authority and do not set the entity's accounting policy.

## Card A — Income statement / P&L

Confirm entity, period (month/quarter/year/YTD), basis, currency and comparative scope. Map revenue, cost of revenue, operating expenses, other income/expense and tax captions from the supplied approved chart/mapping; retain unmapped accounts as exceptions. Recompute total revenue, gross profit, operating income and net income only where component balances exist. Present current vs prior/budget values, absolute delta, percentage formula/denominator, and margins as ratios; label margin change in percentage points. Do not label a variance “material” without a supplied threshold or always-comment policy.

## Card B — Balance sheet

Use ending balances at the stated date, not period activity. Apply approved account grouping for current/noncurrent assets/liabilities and equity; preserve ambiguous/unmapped accounts. Recompute subtotals and total assets and compare with liabilities plus equity. Quantify any difference; never plug. Tie cash, AR/AP, deferred revenue, debt, fixed assets/prepaids and equity to their supporting schedules only when those schedules are supplied and period-aligned.

## Card C — Cash flow statement

First establish beginning and ending cash and whether restricted cash is included under the supplied policy. Use the entity's approved direct/indirect presentation method. For an indirect-method draft, bridge net income with supported noncash adjustments and working-capital changes, then investing and financing movements. Tie net change to beginning-to-ending cash and ending cash to the balance sheet. If cash activity or adjustment schedules are missing, prepare supported P&L/BS sections and mark only the cash-flow statement conclusion blocked; do not derive a cash-flow statement from P&L alone.

## Card D — Comparisons and presentation

Align periods, entities, currency, basis, account mapping and units before comparison. Mark values as source facts or derived totals; add source refs to material lines and retain the mapping table. Include required summary metrics only where inputs support them. State any missing tax, consolidation, cash-flow or adjustment inputs. Do not claim GAAP/IFRS compliance from formatting, and route current presentation/accounting interpretations to the external accountant.

## Card E — Consolidated/multi-entity data

Prepare separate entity views first. Use only approved consolidation and intercompany-elimination entries/schedules. Show the source of each elimination and resulting consolidated total; unresolved intercompany differences remain visible. Do not invent acquisition-date, consolidation, currency translation or elimination policy.
