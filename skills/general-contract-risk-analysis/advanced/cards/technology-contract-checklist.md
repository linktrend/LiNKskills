# Technology-contract checklist

## Trigger

Use when the supplied agreement concerns SaaS/cloud hosting, APIs, AI/model services, software development, technology licensing, or managed technical services. Apply only the relevant agreement-family branches; this is a checklist, not a compliance certification.

## Agreement family and evidence

Identify the supplied MSA, DPA, SOW/order form, SLA, NDA, EULA, API terms, support terms, security exhibit, and IP assignment. Record versions and document precedence. For each material issue, cite the exact section and any conflicting incorporated term. Do not assume missing attachments were provided.

## Issue prompts

- **Service scope and changes:** defined services, dependencies, acceptable use, API quotas/rate limits, version/deprecation notice, change orders, measurable acceptance, support channels and response targets.
- **Availability and remedies:** uptime measurement window, exclusions, reporting source, service credits, chronic-failure rights, maintenance notice, disaster recovery, and whether credits are the sole remedy. Do not supply an unstated “standard” percentage.
- **Data and models:** customer data/prompt/output ownership or license, permitted service use, model training or product-improvement use, retention, deletion, backup copies, subprocessors, export, incident notice, and restrictions on sensitive data. Distinguish what the text says from actual product behavior that needs owner confirmation.
- **Security and audit:** stated safeguards, assurance reports, scope/period of evidence, remediation, audit limits, access controls, incident cooperation, and allocation of investigation costs. Do not imply a control exists merely because the contract references a framework.
- **Intellectual property:** customer deliverables, background tools, vendor platform, generated output, feedback, third-party components, open-source obligations, infringement claims and remedies. Separate a license from ownership and flag undefined residual-use language.
- **Suspension and exit:** suspension triggers and notice, cure path, continuity, data export format/window, deletion confirmation, transition assistance, dependency on other services, and post-termination rights.
- **SOW and delivery:** acceptance criteria, objective tests, dependencies, change-order approval, milestone payments, rework, and ownership/license treatment of deliverables.

## Output and routing

For each relevant issue provide clause locator, source wording, practical effect, which party bears the risk, missing technical/business fact, and a negotiable option if requested. Route a supplied playbook comparison to `legal-commercial-review`; route proposed clause alternatives to `legal-commercial-review-proposals`; route substantive current-law questions separately. Keep the source agreement unchanged and do not negotiate, send, approve, or alter a contract system.
