# Five-clause initial screen

## Trigger and limit

Use as a first-pass screen when the requester wants a concise explanation of a supplied agreement and no organization playbook supplies the controlling position. It is a triage lens, not a substitute for the full contract review. Do not use source examples as current law, universal market practice, company policy, or an automatic risk rating.

## Procedure

For every applicable clause, quote or summarize only the necessary text, record the section/page locator, and capture the known party position. Mark the clause present, absent, unclear, or not applicable. State the practical question and missing fact before drafting an option.

1. **Liability limits.** Identify each cap formula, covered claims, cap period/basis, exclusions, and carve-outs. Compare who is capped and how exceptions interact. Do not call an amount adequate or inadequate without a supplied business scale, playbook, or current authority.
2. **Indemnity.** Identify who protects whom, covered claims and triggers, claim notice, defense control, settlement consent, exclusions, and whether the liability cap applies. Distinguish first-party payment obligations from third-party claim handling.
3. **Intellectual property.** Separate deliverables, pre-existing/background materials, improvements, customer inputs, outputs, licenses, and third-party/open-source material. Flag ownership or license language that is missing or ambiguous; do not infer assignment from payment alone.
4. **Data protection and security.** Identify data categories, roles as described by the contract, permitted use, security commitments, incidents, subprocessors, transfers, retention, return/deletion, and audit evidence. Ask for actual data-flow facts. Route statutory applicability and current-law questions to an appropriate legal research task.
5. **Termination and exit.** Identify termination rights, notice/cure conditions, convenience rights, fees/refunds, transition help, access suspension, and post-termination treatment of data and licenses. Check cross-references to renewal and survival.

## Output

Return a five-row clause table with `status`, `evidence locator`, `plain-language effect`, `open fact/authority`, and `draft option if useful`. Follow with any immediate cross-clause interaction and one sentence stating what this quick screen did not cover. Continue to the full review for requested or material agreement-specific clauses.
