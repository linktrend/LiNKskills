# Market breadth, distribution, follow-through, and market-top successor proposal v0.1.1

Status: source-completion proposal for owner review, not canonical or behavior-qualified. It restores group 7 of the approved spec: “Market breadth, distribution, follow-through and market-top analysis.” The frozen skill itself says its referenced breadth/uptrend/chart support is needed to resolve variants and closure remains incomplete. This proposal defines separate, traceable method families; it does not merge their source-specific definitions into a universal score, recommend an exposure band, or claim an empirical edge.

## Required input contract

Common metadata for every supplied series: instrument/index identifier and exchange; trading-session date and timezone; source and source timestamp; currency/units where applicable; named universe when the series is a member ratio; raw values or source identity; transformation/version; adjustment convention relevant to that series; session calendar; and available history window. Inputs are family-specific: for close-based breadth ratios, provide point-in-time universe membership/effective dates, inclusion/delisting rules, close and required moving-average history, valid-member denominator and missing/suspended/IPO handling; OHLCV is not required for this ratio family. For distribution-day or FTD rules, provide index or named proxy daily OHLCV, volume conventions and a complete session sequence spanning the lookback/event window. For market-top factors, provide only the chosen factor series (for example leadership, sector-relative performance, index moving averages, volatility/sentiment) with source, basis and date. Use a benchmark only when reporting relative returns or cross-asset comparison; the named point-in-time universe is the denominator/context for close-based ratio measures. Unknown coverage is not zero. Missing OHLCV/history makes only DD/FTD branch rows unavailable; it does not block a close-based breadth ratio with valid denominators. Missing universe history blocks historical constituent ratios but may still permit a clearly labeled current-only ratio. Full per-family requirements are consolidated in references/breadth-definitions.md.

## Required output contract

Return a dated evidence report with: (1) raw counts and ratios with numerator/denominator; (2) separate metric-family rows and exact definition/version; (3) event state/date and all boundary inputs for distribution-day or FTD rules; (4) divergence/leadership observations; (5) a clearly labeled source-variant comparison where definitions disagree; (6) supported charts or chart specifications marked not generated; (7) invalid/missing data, lookback, universe and survivorship limitations; (8) non-causal interpretation and counterevidence. Assign an independent status per family and per requested output: complete from supplied evidence, partial with named missing rows, or unavailable with the blocking input. Never fail the whole breadth report because one independent family lacks data. Do not emit the source repositories’ composite score, fixed warning/exposure bands, or backtest claims. Jane may recommend an increase/trim/exit proposal or target/risk level when requested and evidence, holdings and constraints support it; the recommendation is advisory and does not submit an order. Any material/live/capital change requires Lisa and Carlos independent approval channels for the exact version.

## Integrated methods; keep branches distinct

1. **Breadth level and trend.** For each point-in-time universe U and session t, compute a ratio only from eligible securities with valid price history at t: count(U meeting the named predicate at t) / count(U with valid required observations at t). Always publish both counts and the ratio, valid coverage / total eligible universe, filters and as-of membership. A ratio using current constituents is explicitly current-survivor-only and cannot be called historical market breadth. Report advance/decline, new highs/lows, or other measures only when supplied with compatible definitions. Preserve the source dataset’s named breadth index and smoothing values as separately labeled series; do not equate an index normalized to a particular scale with a percentage of stocks.

2. **Uptrend stock ratios and charts.** Preserve distinct predicates for close above 20-, 50-, and 200-session simple moving averages; disclose price adjustment, SMA calculation window, exchange/universe and coverage. The pinned uptrend support derives its universe from a Finviz Elite screener, uses smoothed ratios and directional/sector summaries, and then overlays source-specific composite/exposure bands. Only its raw predicate definitions and ratio concepts are carried here. Finviz membership, historical CSVs, 10-day smoothing, scoring weights, thresholds, and rotation/exposure guidance are not available by assumption. The separate breadth-chart source has an S&P 500 200-day breadth-index visualization and a US uptrend-stock-ratio chart; don’t splice these series or threshold axes. Chart supplied series independently and annotate the as-of, source and denominator.

3. **Distribution-day event rows.** Keep per-session event decisions explainable: return basis and calculation; prior/current volume comparison; candidate event; age in trading sessions; expiry/invalidation state; exact boundary and later invalidation session. One pinned rule uses a daily index/proxy close decline of at least 0.2% with volume greater than prior session, tracks a 25-session window, and invalidates on a later gain of 5% from the event close; its high-versus-close invalidation option and inclusive age-25 convention are versioned semantics. Another market-top guide also discusses stalling days as half counts, while the IBD monitor explicitly excludes them. Show the chosen source convention and, if useful, a separate alternate count; never silently turn half-counts into full counts. The source’s clusters/risk labels are unvalidated heuristics and are not adopted as Jane thresholds.

4. **Follow-through-day sequence.** Require an identified correction, index/proxy, swing-low rule, first rally day, complete session sequence, OHLCV, and current day count. Preserve source variants separately. The pinned FTD method defines a qualifying correction as at least 3% from a recent high with at least three down sessions; swing low is based on closing price; it defines Day 1 after the low, requires rally integrity, and accepts a day 4–10 event with at least 1.25% gain and volume above the prior session. The market-top/chart guide instead emphasizes day 4–7 and at least 1.5% gain. The sources differ in correction/rally conditions and gain/window boundary. Provide per-variant predicates and exact qualifying dates, or mark variant evaluation unavailable; do not pick a “correct” universal threshold. Source-published success/failure percentages and quality/action scores have no local validation here and are excluded.

5. **Market-top evidence.** Present a factor ledger, not a single top detector: (a) distribution events/clustering; (b) leadership deterioration; (c) defensive versus cyclical/leading sector rotation; (d) index-versus-breadth divergence with explicit high-relative-to-window definition; (e) index price/moving-average structure; (f) sentiment/speculation such as VIX/put-call only when supplied and instrument definitions are verified. For every row state supporting and contradicting observations, lookback, source, and stale/missing inputs. Do not claim that a top has been detected from weights, a numerical cutoff, an anecdotal historical analog, or one unverified source. A market-top risk assessment must distinguish topping risk from a correction already underway and from bottom/FTD analysis.

6. **Conclusion.** State what the measures show, where the families agree/disagree, whether data coverage supports comparison, what evidence would falsify the view, and next observation to monitor. Historical performance, predictive probability, allocation targets and trade recommendations require independent point-in-time data, reproducible implementation and out-of-sample validation; source documentation is not validation.
