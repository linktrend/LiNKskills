# Old patterns rejected

- Claiming an incident is closed from narrative without closure evidence.
- Treating backup existence as proof of restore or recovery readiness.
- Deploying, rolling back, isolating, or rotating credentials from a skill.
- Sending internal or customer communication from a draft helper.
- Copying customer records, credentials, private incident text, or raw logs into
  release artifacts or telemetry.
- Mutating a Program Ledger, deployment system, incident tracker, or scheduler.
