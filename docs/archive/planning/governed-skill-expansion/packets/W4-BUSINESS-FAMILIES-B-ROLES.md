# Wave 4 — Business Families B and Role Manifests

## PKT-18 — Meeting Management

**Depends on:** PKT-06.

Evaluate maintained meeting candidates. Cover agenda, private pre-brief, notes/transcript processing, decisions/commitments/tasks, routing references to Google/agent stores, and follow-up verification. Do not retain raw private transcripts in releases or telemetry.

## PKT-19 — Procurement and Vendor Management

**Depends on:** PKT-17.

Cover supplier comparison, claim/pricing verification, contract/renewal/performance tracking, concentration/continuity risk, and approval briefs before commitment. Spending, acceptance, and vendor credentials remain consumer authority.

## PKT-20 — Agent Workforce Management

**Depends on:** PKT-13.

Cover reusable-role definition, applicable Brain-rule selection, proper capability/skill assignment requests, domain delegation, workload/blocker/evidence monitoring, quality/repeated-failure evaluation, training/skill/authority recommendations, and safe suspend/retire proposals. Never copy credentials/private memory, activate agents, or approve technical grants.

## PKT-21 — Company Incident and Continuity Management

**Depends on:** PKT-07, PKT-13.

Cover outage/security coordination, backup/recovery decisions, continuity, internal/customer communication, evidence capture, and closure. Preserve owning responder, Platform, Program Ledger, and deployment authority; closure requires evidence, not narrative.

## PKT-22 — Reusable role-pack manifests

**Depends on:** PKT-05 and PKT-09–PKT-21.

Publish five immutable reference-only manifests:

- Lisa CEO: governance, planning, supervision/delegation/evidence, workforce, incident, and relevant cross-domain understanding; private jobs remain instance bindings.
- Eric CTO: development, technical operations, IDE/LiNKdeveloper Program, architecture/release/incident; cannot approve own changes.
- David CPO: product/market/feasibility, strategy/requirements, launch/marketing/acquisition, service/success, named Program operations, approved stack skills.
- Sara COO/CFO: operations, finance/Odoo, agent operations, provider/Librarian standards, procurement/continuity, interim legal, eligible Workspace references.
- Jane Chief Trading Officer: LiNKtrading operations, strategy/risk governance/reporting; no manual/individual-trade skill.

**Acceptance:** Every reference is an exact qualified release; missing/incompatible/unqualified references fail validation; manifests contain no skill body, identity, credential, activation, live pin, private data, or account binding; future agents remain inactive.

## Common acceptance and evidence

Packets 18–21 meet the common business-family acceptance in Wave 3. PKT-22 adds manifest-schema tests, exact dependency-closure tests, capability-class declarations, role-applicability tests, and negative activation/credential fixtures.
