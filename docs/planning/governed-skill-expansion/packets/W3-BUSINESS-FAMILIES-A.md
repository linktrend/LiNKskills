# Wave 3 — Business Families A

Each packet first inventories existing LiNKskills and reviewed external candidates, records reuse/adapt/new decisions, and uses the external lifecycle for imported material. Each release includes authoritative-tool contracts without credentials or connectors.

## PKT-13 — Executive Decisions and Governance

**Depends on:** PKT-06, PKT-08.

Build concise matter/evidence/risk/options/recommendation briefs; always support `Other — specify`; record approved decision/rule impact and implementation tracking without granting authority. Reuse executive/supervision primitives where non-overlapping. Accept only evidence-honest, mobile-readable decisions with unique references.

## PKT-14 — Company Planning and Performance

**Depends on:** PKT-13.

Cover quarterly, annual, three-year, five-year, rolling four-week, and monthly planning; objectives/KPIs; forecast versus actual; late/blocked/obsolete detection; and evidence-backed reprioritization. Avoid false precision and duplicate mutable Program/Task state.

## PKT-15 — Finance and Accounting Operations

**Depends on:** PKT-03.

Evaluate Anthropic small-business candidates and official Odoo contracts/licences. Cover cash-flow forecasting, budget/actual, invoice/payment/expense tracking, close preparation, runway/risk, and approval boundaries. Target the approved Odoo tool/API contract; do not create a ledger, connector, or accounting system.

## PKT-16 — Sales and Customer Management

**Depends on:** PKT-03.

Cover lead intake/qualification, Odoo pipeline, proposals/follow-up, onboarding, renewals/customer risk, and founder escalation. Preserve LiNKreach customer-service ownership and avoid duplicating CRM/runtime connectors.

## PKT-17 — Commercial Contracts and Legal Operations

**Depends on:** PKT-03.

Evaluate official Anthropic legal sources and licences. Cover intake, plain-English summary, obligations/renewals, approved-playbook comparison, and escalation to lawyer/Principal. Explicitly refuse final legal authority, invented law, unauthorized acceptance, and hidden jurisdiction assumptions.

## Common acceptance and evidence

- Existing-overlap matrix and source/licence/security/maintenance review.
- Complete eval suite with ordinary, ambiguous, adversarial, authority, tool-failure, and privacy cases.
- Exact release/provenance records and declared effects.
- No live Odoo credentials, customer data, contract text, or company private data in fixtures.
- Rollback to prior exact qualified release; no live pointer or activation change without approval.
