## 9. Flexible-period binding

The period remains work unless, at `14:45`, all are true:

1. no overdue Principal work;
2. no suitable ready current-week task;
3. no resolvable blocker;
4. no deadline or weekly outcome at risk; and
5. no useful work action reasonably needed before the next workday.

Four-week visibility alone does not consume the period. A later change requires genuinely new important work.
